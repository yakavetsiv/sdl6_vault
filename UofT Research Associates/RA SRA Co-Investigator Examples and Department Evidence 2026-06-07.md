# RA SRA Co-Investigator Examples and Department Evidence 2026-06-07

> [!summary]
> Focused note for the question: which U of T departments/examples show co-investigator or co-PI language relevant to Research Associate or Senior Research Associate paths?

## Working Answer
- U of T's central PI eligibility guidance is the controlling reference for the safest argument: **Research Associate = not PI eligible; Senior Research Associate = Co-I only**.
- The RA/SRA policy allows a **Senior Research Associate** to act as co-investigator with a faculty member in exceptional circumstances when chair/dean approval and funder rules permit it.
- Public job/profile examples show co-investigator language in health/pharmacy and education-language research contexts, but these examples should be used as supporting evidence, not as a guarantee of eligibility.

## Department / Unit Examples
- **Leslie Dan Faculty of Pharmacy**: 2026 Research Associate (Limited Term), Req ID 47222, describes a senior scientific leadership role in Dr. Sara Guilcher's OPTI-HEx Lab. The posting includes grant application work "as co-PI or Co-Investigator," multi-investigator proposal coordination, collaborator relationships, publication/dissemination, and trainee supervision.
- **OISE / CERLL / Department of Language Studies, UTM**: Simone Casini profile lists Senior Research Associate status and co-investigator role on an SSHRC-funded project, showing an education/language-research example of SRA-adjacent co-investigator language.
- **Central research administration**: U of T PI eligibility guidance states that SRA roles can be Co-I only, which makes SRA the cleaner policy-aligned comparator than ordinary Research Associate when arguing co-investigator eligibility.

## How To Use This In HR Framing
- Do not argue that the current USW role automatically becomes PI-eligible or Co-I-eligible.
- Use the SRA/co-investigator pathway as evidence that U of T recognizes a higher level of staff research responsibility beyond routine technical/admin support.
- Stronger phrasing: "The revised duties resemble advanced independent research responsibility, grant/protocol contribution, collaborator-facing scientific leadership, and research-output ownership. U of T's SRA framework recognizes this kind of higher-level contribution through exceptional Co-I eligibility, while USW job evaluation should still determine the appropriate USW band for this role."
