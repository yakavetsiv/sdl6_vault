# UofT Research Associates and Senior Research Associates

> [!summary]
> Official-source corpus for University of Toronto Research Associates (Limited Term) and Senior Research Associates: governing policies, salary ranges, performance assessment, compensation, leave/vacation, immigration, and related policy pages.

## Main Takeaways
- U of T policy defines two research-position categories: **Research Associate (Limited Term)** and **Senior Research Associate**.
- Normal qualifications are an advanced degree such as PhD, MD, or equivalent in the field of research.
- Senior Research Associate positions are continuing positions where grant funding is expected to continue; Research Associate (Limited Term) appointments are limited-term and renewable to a total of five years.
- RA/SRA work is academic in nature, but RA/SRA staff are not members of the teaching staff.
- RA/SRA positions should be used where the individual carries out research responsibilities with a high degree of independence under the direction of the principal investigator.
- RA/SRA employees are included in U of T's annual SuccessFactors performance-assessment and goal-setting cycle for non-represented staff groups.
- The 2025 salary range document lists Research Associates (Limited Term) at minimum **$53,520** and maximum **$100,350**; Senior Research Associates at minimum **$62,617**, breakpoint **$109,373**, and notional maximum **$127,112**.
