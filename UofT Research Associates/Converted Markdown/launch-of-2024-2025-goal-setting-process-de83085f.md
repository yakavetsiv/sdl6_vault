 Launch of 2024/2025 Goal Setting Process 

 From:  Jason Lum, Director, Compensation 

 Dear colleagues, 
 The 2024/2025 Goal Setting process is now open for Professional & Managerial (PM), Confidential (C), and Research Associate / Senior Research Associate (RA/SRA) employees. 
 Employees in the PM, Confidential and RA/SRA employee groups are invited to record their goals and professional development activities in the  2025 Goal Plan  of the  SuccessFactors system  for the July 1, 2024 to June 30, 2025 review period.  Please note that Goal setting process is mandatory for PM, Confidential and RA / SRA employees. 
 To support employees in reaching their 2025 goals, the Centre for Learning, Leadership & Culture (LLC) has Individual Development Plan (IDP) tools and resources to guide and document meaningful conversations about any development opportunities that would be beneficial. IDPs are a joint exercise between employees and their managers to identify opportunities to develop the employee’s current role and support career aspirations. 
 See our Frequently Asked Questions below for more information. If you have any additional questions, please contact your  Divisional HR Office . 
 Logging in to SuccessFactors  
 Please  log in to SuccessFactors  using your UTORid and password. It is recommended that you bookmark this link for future use. 
 You can also access SuccessFactors at  https://uoft.me/successfactors . 
 How do I record my Goals and Professional Development Activities in the Performance & Goals module?  
 The SuccessFactors: Performance & Goals module (PMGM) training demos & materials are available  in the HR Service Centre . 
 Where do I learn more about Individual Development Plans? 
 The LLC provides the tools and resources to help employees and managers put individual development plans together. Visit the Development section of the LLC web site to get started, or take the Development Planning for Employees course to learn more. 
 Who do I contact if I have any issues?  
 Goal Setting Process Support:  
 If you have any questions about the Goal Setting process or require assistance regarding your goals/professional development activities, please contact your  Divisional HR office . 
 What if my Manager information is incorrect?  
 Any inquiries about the reporting relationship records should be directed to your  Divisional HR Office  for correction. 
 I am having a technical issue entering my goals – who should I contact?  
 If you are experiencing any technical issues, please contact  pmgm.help@utoronto.ca  for assistance.
