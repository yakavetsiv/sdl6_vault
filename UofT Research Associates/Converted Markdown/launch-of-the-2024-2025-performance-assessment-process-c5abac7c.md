 Launch of the 2024/2025 Performance Assessment Process 

 By:  Jason Lum, Director, Compensation 

 Dear colleagues, 
 The 2024/2025 Performance Assessment process begins today, Tuesday, April 1 for all Professional & Managerial employees, Confidential employees, Research Associates and Senior Research Associates via the SuccessFactors: Performance platform. 
 The Annual Performance Assessment Process 
 Our process is designed to support employees’ potential while formally acknowledging individual performances relative to their manager’s expectations and departmental, divisional, and organizational goals. 
 All eligible employees are encouraged to have a fulsome review with their managers regarding their contributions and development plans. 
 Process Timeline & Next Steps 
 The process timeline, support resources, and further information about each step of the Performance Assessment process can be found in the HR Service Centre . 
 The review period for Performance Assessment is July 1, 2024 to June 30, 2025 for Professional & Managerial, Confidential staff and Research and Senior Research Associates. 
 The first step of the process, the Employee Activity Report, must be submitted to your Reviewer by Thursday, April 17. 
 Questions? 
 If you have any questions regarding this year’s Performance Assessment process, please contact your Divisional HR Office . 
 Please send any SuccessFactors-related questions to pmgm.help@utoronto.ca .
