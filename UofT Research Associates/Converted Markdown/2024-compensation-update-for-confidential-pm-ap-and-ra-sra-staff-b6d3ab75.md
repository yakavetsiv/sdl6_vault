 Compensation Memos 

 2024 Compensation update for Confidential, PM, AP and RA / SRA staff 

 From:  Kelly Hannah-Moffat, VP, People Strategy, Equity & Culture 

 To: Professional & Managerial Staff; Confidential Staff; Advancement Professionals; Research Associates & Senior Research Associates 

 On September 27, 2023, the Business Board approved a series of recommendations regarding compensation increases for Confidential, Professional & Managerial (PM), Advancement Professionals (AP), and Research Associate & Senior Research Associate (RA / SRA) staff. 
 Merit increases   
 The University administers annual merit increases for eligible non-represented staff groups in line with each group’s existing compensation policies. Merit increases will be determined based on June 30, 2024 salaries and will be implemented in the July 2024 pay. 
 Across the Board (ATB) increase   
 Across the Board (ATB) increases for eligible non-represented staff groups have historically been determined by taking into account the compensation increases negotiated with some of our other employee groups. Accordingly, the Business Board has approved a 2% ATB increase for eligible employees effective July 1, 2024. 
 Reminders   
 The deadline to complete and submit the activity report for each employee group is below. If you have any questions, please contact: pmgm.help@utoronto.ca . 

 April 19 for Professional Managerial, Confidential and Research or Senior Research Associates 
 May 30 for Advancement Professionals 

 Processing Instructions   
 Detailed processing instructions for this year’s merit increases will be distributed to Divisional HR Offices and Business Officers in advance of the July pay close. 
 Should you have any questions, please contact your Divisional HR Office .
