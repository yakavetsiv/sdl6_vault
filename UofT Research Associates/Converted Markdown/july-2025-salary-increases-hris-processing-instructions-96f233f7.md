 July 2025 Salary Increases for Appointed Non-Represented and USW Staff Groups – HRIS Processing Instructions 

 From:  Sheryl Vitug, Senior HRIS Training & Project Specialist  

 To: PSEC Staff; Divisional HR Offices; Business Officers 

 In accordance with the Compensation update for Confidential, PM, AP and RA / SRA staff memo , and USW Local 1998 – Staff Appointed Memorandum of Agreement , the following staff groups will have salary increases effective July 1, 2025. 

 Faculty / Staff Group 
 Salary Increase 

 PM (1-5), Confidential, Research Associate (RA), Sr. Research Associate (SRA) 

 1.8% ATB increase  and, 
 Merit Increase based on June 30, 2025 salary 

 PM (6-11) / Advancement Professionals (AP) 

 Merit Increase based on June 30, 2025 salary 

 Appointed USW 

 1.8% ATB increase based on June 30, 2025 salary 

 Please be advised that all salary increases must be entered into the HRIS system no later than the July 23 monthly payroll close. 
 To support this process, the following reports must be generated both prior to and after the salary increases are processed, covering the reporting period from July 1, 2025, to December 31, 9999: 

 HR Data Verification Report as of July 1, 2025 
 Appointment Details Report – ZHSQAPPTDETL 
 Basic Pay Report – ZHSQBASICPAY 
 Absence Summary Report (Paid and Unpaid Long Term Absence) 

 Please note: Any appointment changes, grid processions, or CC/CFC updates effective July 1, 2025, or later must be processed after the salary increases have been completed. This sequencing is essential to minimize exceptions and ensure the accuracy of employee records. 
 Reference Guides 
 1. PM, Confidential, RA, SRA and AP 
 The Fast Entry Upload for Basic Pay and Appointment Detail Infotypes 
 The Fast Entry Upload Processing Guide provides step-by-step instructions for updating the Basic Pay Infotype (IT0008) and the Appointment Detail Infotype (IT9009) using the Salary Increase Template . This process applies to eligible staff groups, excluding individuals with exceptions on their records. 
 For employees with exceptions that cannot be processed through the Salary Increase Template, the accompanying chart outlines various scenarios along with the appropriate reference guides to follow.

 2. Centralized ATB Increase for USW Appointed Staff – July 9 
 The Across-the-Board (ATB) salary increase for USW Appointed staff will be processed centrally on July 9 at 5:00 p.m. , excluding employees with exceptions on their records. 
 Departments are required to run the USW ATB Exception and Update Report both before and after the central update. For employees excluded from the automated process due to exceptions, please consult the USW Exception Descriptions and Corrective Action Reference Chart to determine the appropriate transactions to be executed manually. 
 The chart outlines various exception scenarios along with the corresponding reference guides to support accurate and timely processing.

 The July 2025 Timeline for ATB Increase for Appointed USW and all resources listed below are available on the HR Systems & Training Support Centre . 

 HRIS Support – July Salary Increase Information Session & One-on-One Assistance 
 The HRIS Support Team will be offering the following opportunities to assist with July salary increase process: 

 A July Salary Increase Information Session for Business Officers / Payroll Officers will be available on July 8, 10:30 am – 11:30 am. Register through the LMS to join the event. 
 Get Help for July Increases One-on-One support sessions can be booked by submitting a ticket through HR Service Centre before July 21. 

 Questions and Contact Information 
 Questions with respect to eligibility for Appointed Staff Groups should be directed to your  Divisional HR Office . 
 For USW, Central Payroll Services will update the Basic Pay IT0008 for those employees receiving a Supplemental Unemployment Benefit (SUB) on July 1 for an approved leave of absence. Approved leaves of absence include Pregnancy, Parental, Adoption, Primary Caregiver Leave or Compassionate Family Caregiver Leave. Business Officers must update the Appointment Detail (IT9009) to reflect those updates. If the employee is also receiving a grid progression in July, the Department must inform Central Payroll Services by July 21 payroll forms / changes deadline. 
 If you need assistance with HRIS processing, please submit a ticket to HRIS Help Desk using the HR Service Centre.
