# SDL6 Highlights — February 2026

## LiveCount (now VibeCount)

Alternative to commercial cell counters (~$250k/unit). Automated cell counter using current microscope imaging system + ML.

- **Lead:** Ilya Yakavets
- **Dataset:** Rosanna Jiang
- **Goal:** Higher accuracy across multiple cell types than current AI-assisted market tools

## Microtissue Contractility Analysis

Developed by Ilya Yakavets and Yimu Zhao with good accuracy.

## ADDR Review Published

SDL6 review accepted and online version published.
