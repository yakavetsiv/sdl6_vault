# SDL HOM — Research Objectives & CFI V2 Rationale
**Source:** `OneDrive/.../SDL6 - HOM/Data (secure)/Grants/CFI 2024/SDL HOM.docx`  
**Type:** Internal SDL-HOM research function document  

---

## Research Objectives (RO)
- **RO1:** Development and validation of high-fidelity organ-on-chip and organoid models mimicking human tissues
- **RO2:** High-throughput screening of drug candidates for safety, efficacy, and pharmacodynamics
- **RO3:** Integration of AI/ML to analyze experimental data, identify biomarkers, predict clinical responses

## V1 Status (CFREF-funded, complete Dec 2025)
- Autonomous passaging and monitoring of adherent and suspended static cell cultures
- Minimum dispensing volume: several microliters
- Screening: high-throughput colorimetric and luminescence assays; medium-throughput imaging
- Limitations: no high-content imaging, no proteomics, no flow cytometry (addressed via core facilities)

> [!WARNING] V1 Limitations
> V1 lacks high-content imaging, proteomics, and flow cytometry. Each V2 instrument directly addresses one of these gaps — this V1/V2 comparison is the core justification structure for the CFI equipment request.

## V2 Rationale (CFI-funded)
- Addresses V1 screening bottlenecks
- Boosts throughput
- Expands assay range
- Adds: high-content confocal imaging, flow cytometry, expanded automation

## Innovation Statement
> "By utilizing advanced organ-on-chip and organoid technologies to recapitulate human biology in a high-fidelity, high-throughput manner, our autonomous platform aims to streamline the entire workflow."

## Relevance to Equipment Justification
- Directly supports V2 equipment request narrative
- V1 limitations justify each V2 instrument
- RO3 (AI integration) justifies imaging plate reader as AI feedback loop node
