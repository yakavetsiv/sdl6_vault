## Summary
Pan-Canadian Drug Discovery SDL Network proposal connecting U of T, UBC, Université de Montréal, and UHN. Establishes distributed SDL network spanning synthesis, medicinal chemistry, formulation, and biological screening.

> [!INFO] Network Positioning
> SDL6-HOM sits within this pan-Canadian network as the human tissue screening node, providing the biological validation endpoint downstream of synthesis and formulation SDLs.

## Key Points
- Drug discovery bottlenecks addressed: hit-to-lead optimization, formulation, preclinical models, parallel compound evaluation
- SDL paradigm: AI prediction → robotic synthesis/characterization → AI analysis → next experiment (closed loop)
- SDL6-HOM sits within this network as the human tissue screening node

## SDL Network Nodes (from document)
- **Organic Synthesis & Feasibility** (UofT): synthetic routes, hit optimization building blocks
- **Medicinal Chemistry SDL** (UHN): hit-to-lead optimization, efficacy/selectivity/safety profiling
- **SDL6-HOM**: human organ mimicry, preclinical screening with organoids/organ-on-chip

## Relevance to Equipment Justification
- Establishes CFI context: HOM SDL is node in pan-Canadian network
- Justifies "no existing Canadian platform" claim in equipment section
- Drug delivery and nanomedicine screening positioned as critical gap
