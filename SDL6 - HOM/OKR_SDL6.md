## Objective
Build a SDL that can accelerate drug screening and disease modeling with high precision and high clinical relevance.

## Background — Why Organ-on-Chip/Organoids
1. Human-relevant microenvironment: cell-cell interactions, ECM dynamics, biomechanical forces
2. Full automation compatibility: high-content imaging, multi-omics, ML-driven insights
3. Regulatory push: NIH 21st Century Cures Act 2.0, Modernization Act 3.0 — human models over animal testing

## Key Gaps Addressed
- Assay reliability and reproducibility across labs
- Throughput limitations in culture and data acquisition
- Biological complexity: immune components, neural interactions, vascularization missing from current models

> [!NOTE] Regulatory Tailwind
> NIH 21st Century Cures Act 2.0 and Modernization Act 3.0 explicitly push for human models over animal testing — this regulatory context strengthens the case for organ-on-chip/organoid infrastructure in grant applications.

## OKRs

### Objective 1: Fully autonomous high-fidelity 2D/3D cell culture
- KR1.1: Human labour 10 h/week/sample → 1-2 h/week
- KR1.2: Walk-away time <1 day (manual) → >30 days (autonomous)
- KR1.3: Autonomous maintenance of ≥10 distinct cell types (iPSC-derived, patient-derived)
- KR1.4: Confluency CV >20% → <15%; seeded cell count CV 20% → <10%

### Objective 2: Richer data per biological sample
- KR2.1: Feature extraction 2-3 metrics → >100 quantitative features (morphology, texture, granularity)
- KR2.2: Live monitoring >24 h/week/sample automated
- KR2.3: ≥2 multimodal readouts (imaging + flow cytometry + molecular staining)

### Objective 3: Enhanced preclinical drug screening predictive capacity
- KR3.1: ≥3 organ-on-chip/organoid models validated vs. human tissue complexity
- KR3.2: Case study of false-positive/negative drugs in preclinical assessment
- KR3.3: Positive alignment between in vitro organ-on-chip data and clinical trial outcomes

### Objective 4: Reproducibility & standardization
- KR4.1: ≥10 cell culture protocols automated, experimental variability -30% vs. manual
- KR4.2: ML-enabled QC pipeline with live-cell imaging feedback loop
- KR4.3: Benchmarking study vs. traditional methods

### Objective 5: AI-driven therapeutic development
- KR5.1: Structured ML-ready dataset from ≥50 organ-on-chip experiments
- KR5.2: AI models predicting IC50/EC50 from organ-on-chip data
- KR5.3: ≥3 biotech/pharma collaborations using AI-enhanced screening platform
