# SDL-HOM Facility Overview & Access Policy
**Source:** `OneDrive/.../SDL6 - HOM/Reports/SDL-access.docx`  
**Type:** Facility overview and user access document  

---

## Mission
Autonomous AI-assisted platform for culturing and screening high-fidelity models of functional tissues and diseases. Accelerates discovery of next-generation tissue models and novel therapies; reduces animal testing reliance.

## Core Capabilities
- Cell passaging and sample preparation
- Closed-loop optimization of culture conditions (growth media, ECM support)
- Automated dataset generation
- Reproducible cell batches: patient-derived organoids (PDOs), differentiated iPSCs
- Workflows: static and dynamic co-culture organ-on-chip (OOC), colony picking, bioprinting

## Biological Materials Supported
Small molecules, biomacromolecules, **nanomedicines**, hydrogels, cells

## Location
Terrence Donnelly Centre for Cellular & Biomolecular Research (CCBR), University of Toronto

## Access Model
- Collaborative research agreements
- Pay-per-use pricing
- Fees recover: consumables, maintenance, repairs, staff expertise
- Supported by full-time AC staff scientists

## 2025–2026 Priority Focus
> "Demonstrate the value of the self-driving lab (closed-loop) approach compared to conventional high-throughput research. Focus on organoids, organ-on-chip and other complex in vitro models."

> [!WARNING] Integrated System Access Constraint
> Equipment in SDL-HOM is part of an integrated system — not all instruments will be accessible individually and may only be available when not in use by integrated workflows. Factor this into user onboarding and scheduling.

## Important Access Note
> "Since equipment in SDL-HOM is part of an integrated system, not all equipment will be accessible individually and may only be available when not used by integrated workflows."

## Relevance to CFI Application
- Confirms nanomedicine as explicit use case
- Supports CFI "need for infrastructure" argument (integrated system, not individual instruments)
