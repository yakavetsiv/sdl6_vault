Robotic Automation Advisor — Autonomous
Validation Lab

Company: TakaHuman LLC (US)
Location: Remote (US) / Hybrid preferred (California)

Company Vision & Background

TakaHuman LLC is a U.S.-based company founded by Dan Takahashi, a serial entrepreneur with two
prior successful exits including a recent IPO on the Tokyo Stock Exchange.

Our long-term vision is to create a new higher level of humanity — extending healthspan, elevating
cognitive and physical performance, and enabling seamless integration between biological and digital
intelligence.

Our mission is to build a unified scientific AI simulation platform spanning medicine, energy materials,
and semiconductor materials. A core pillar is enabling clients to validate simulation results inside a fully
autonomous robotic lab with no human-in-the-loop.

Role Summary

TakaHuman is hiring a Robotic Automation Advisor to design and help launch our first fully automated
validation lab from stage zero.

Drug development — organ-on-chip, engineered human tissue, multi-organ systems
Battery materials — lithium-ion and next-gen chemistries for EV/BESS
Perovskite solar panels — multi-tandem devices and thin-film optimization

Responsibilities

Architect a fully autonomous robotic laboratory from concept to build plan
Define robotics, automation stack, instrumentation, and safety framework
Evaluate and select lab automation vendors and integration partners
Design closed-loop integration between simulation software and physical experiments
Ensure scalability, reproducibility, uptime, and minimal human oversight
