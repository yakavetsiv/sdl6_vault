4.  Invention Description:

Please provide a description of this invention for evaluation, highlighting its novel or patentable aspects. Attach
separate pages as necessary.

Combination therapies are a cornerstone of cancer treatment, offering enhanced therapeutic efficacy. However,
determining the optimal doses, durations, and sequences of multidrug administration remains a time-consuming
and labor-intensive process. Here, we introduce a novel automated tumor-on-a-chip platform, which uniquely
integrates a breast tumor-on-a-chip microfluidic system with an automated fluid handling module, enabling machine
learning (ML)-driven optimization of drug administration regimens.
This ML-driven platform rapidly identifies the most effective treatment strategies by systematically exploring and
optimizing drug administration sequences. Using this approach, we discovered a clinically approved three-drug
combination with an optimized sequential regimen that significantly reduces the total drug dose compared to
conventional concurrent administration. Similarly, for patient-derived breast cancer organoids, we discovered a
synergistic effect of two co-administered investigational drugs, which diminished when delivered sequentially,
providing critical insights into drug interaction dynamics.
Our platform accelerates multidrug treatment optimization by reducing the number of experimental conditions
required through ML-guided decision-making, offering a highly efficient alternative to traditional trial-and-error
approaches. This fully integrated system represents a significant advancement in the development of personalized
chemotherapies and effective combination treatments for cancer.
