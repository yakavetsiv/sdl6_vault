## Reclassification Relevance
- If significant change is established, the total weighted score could change enough to move the job into a new payband classification.
- The total weighted score might also not increase enough to move the job into a higher payband.
- If the Union disagrees with the University's assessment, it can dispute the decision under the Maintenance Protocol.

## Use in Current Case
- Supports the point that HR/University cannot treat title wording alone as a pay-band change.
- Supports the stronger framing that the position should be re-evaluated against the USW factors and assigned the pay band supported by the total score.
- Supports asking for the appropriate evaluated pay band, not an arbitrary one-band-only increase.
