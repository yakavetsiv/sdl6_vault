# USW Local 1998 Job Evaluation

> [!summary]
> Union overview of USW job evaluation as a systematic review of jobs across the University, based on skill, effort, responsibility, and working conditions, with reclassification available when a job has significantly changed.

## Reclassification Relevance
- Job evaluation compares jobs across the University and determines the appropriate pay band.
- Employees in a position for six months can submit a questionnaire so the Union can further assess duties.
- At U of T staff-appointed, job evaluation is in maintenance stage; reclassification is considered where the job has significantly changed.

## Use in Current Case
- Supports the point that HR/University cannot treat title wording alone as a pay-band change.
- Supports the stronger framing that the position should be re-evaluated against the USW factors and assigned the pay band supported by the total score.
- Supports asking for the appropriate evaluated pay band, not an arbitrary one-band-only increase.
