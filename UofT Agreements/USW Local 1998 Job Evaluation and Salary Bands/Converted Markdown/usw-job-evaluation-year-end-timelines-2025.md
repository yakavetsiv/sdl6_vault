# Job Evaluation Year-End Timelines

From: [Jason Lum, Director, Compensation](https://people.utoronto.ca/author/jasonlum/)

Dear colleagues,

As the holiday season is approaching, we are providing a timeline for job evaluation submissions. Please find the timelines below for USW job evaluation and PM/C, A/P job evaluation.

## **USW Job Evaluation**

| Process | HR Action | Due date | Expectations for JE Services Team |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Conversions for reorgs and reorg planning | Email your JE Analyst. | Friday, November 28 | Please inform your JE Analyst immediately of any upcoming re-orgs that are taking place in 2025/2026. |
| Manager Reclassification | Submit request to MSS, submit analysis to JE Analyst in JDX, ensure the changes to the work meet the test for significant change (see below for more details). | Friday, November 28 | The USW JE Services team will endeavour to work with you until pay close on December 18th. Please note that new positions and conversions for posting are always our priority. We will inform you if we are unable to finalize by pay close. |
| Casual Conversions (Priority New positions) | Submit request to MSS, submit analysis to JE Analyst in JDX.   Submit Pension Inquiry ticket via HRSC for Central Pension to review case. | Wednesday December 3 | Please inform your JE Analyst of casual conversions as soon as possible. Note that UPP requires casual conversions to be classified and implemented by the year-end pay close – **December 18th**.  As such, these will take priority over all other new position requests. |
| New Positions | Submit request to MSS, submit analysis to JE Analyst in JDX. | Wednesday December 3 | We will work with you until the end of day on December 23rd. |
| Conversions to JDX for posting | Email your JE Analyst. | Wednesday December 3 | We will work with you until the end of the day on December 23rd. Between now and the end of the year we will not be able to guarantee the 48-hour turnaround that we normally do. Please plan accordingly. |
| Employee Reclassification |  | Follow timelines set out in USW JE Protocol | Please discuss the timelines of Employee Reclassifications with your JE Analyst if you are unsure. |

**IMPORTANT:** All USW reclassifications, new positions and conversions submitted after the due dates above will be reviewed in January.

As always, the expectation for all new positions and potential reclassifications is that the HR Consultant or Advisor investigates new submissions and works with their client to find the best course of action in advance of submitting work to the USW JE Services Team. This includes assessing significant change for reclassifications. Reclassifications are based on material changes to the job that amount to significant change.

The definition of “significant change” under the USW JE Maintenance Protocol is:

* When the duties or responsibilities of a position are added, removed, or otherwise altered on a **permanent and ongoing basis** and there is a demonstrated change in the skill, effort, responsibility or working conditions required to perform the new or altered duties such that the job no longer falls within its current job class. A mere change in the mix or emphasis of duties and responsibilities is not considered significant change.

The USW JE team is always available for informal consult in advance of submission regarding significant change or placement in a job class.

## **PM/C, A/P Job Evaluation**

|  |  |  |  |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Process | HR Action | Due date | Expectations for Comp Team |
| Professional & Managerial Staff,  Confidentials A/P Job Evaluations | Submit request | Friday November 28 | Please prepare and submit evaluation material to assess if a job evaluation committee can be scheduled before the holiday break (if possible) or soon after the holiday break. We will work with you until the end of day on December 23rd. |

We value our partnership with our HR colleagues and are committed to working with you to meet your clients’ needs during this very busy time of year. Please reach out to your job evaluation contact to discuss further.
