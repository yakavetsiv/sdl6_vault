## Reclassification Relevance
- Each pay band is comprised of a 19-point range; total points place a position in the corresponding pay band.
- Employee-initiated reclassification asks how skill, responsibility, effort, and working conditions significantly changed.
- A distinct change in the nature and character of duties is required; volume or rearrangement of similar duties is not enough.
- Manager-initiated reclassification applies when duties and responsibilities warrant placement in a new job class and pay band.

## Use in Current Case
- Supports the point that HR/University cannot treat title wording alone as a pay-band change.
- Supports the stronger framing that the position should be re-evaluated against the USW factors and assigned the pay band supported by the total score.
- Supports asking for the appropriate evaluated pay band, not an arbitrary one-band-only increase.
