# USW Reclassification Pay Band Movement Sources 2026-06-07

> [!summary]
> Focused source pack for the question: can USW HR add one pay band or multiple pay bands? The public materials support that reclassification is score-driven, not capped at one band in the language reviewed here.

## Working Answer
- A USW position can move to whatever pay band is supported by the evaluated total score; the public materials reviewed here do not state a one-band cap.
- The stronger wording is not "please add one band"; it is "please evaluate the role under the USW factors and place it in the pay band supported by the score and comparators."
- The evidence must show significant change in the nature and character of duties, especially skill, responsibility, effort, and working conditions.
- More workload or a title change alone is weak evidence.
