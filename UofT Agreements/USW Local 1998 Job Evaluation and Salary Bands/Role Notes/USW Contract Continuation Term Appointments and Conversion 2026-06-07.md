# USW Contract Continuation Term Appointments and Conversion 2026-06-07

> [!summary]
> Focused note for the question: if a full-time USW employee continues after one year into a second year, is there an automatic permanent/continuing transition or a need to sign a new contract?

## Working Answer
- The **collective agreement** itself continues automatically after June 30, 2026 for one-year periods unless U of T or USW gives timely notice to amend it. That continuation does **not** itself make an individual employee permanent/continuing.
- For **USW staff-appointed continuing employees**, there is no predetermined end date. There is usually no annual renewal contract to sign just because a second year starts.
- For **USW staff-appointed term employees**, each term appointment has a predetermined end date. If the employer wants the employee to continue into a second year, the continuation should be documented as an extension or new term appointment. Full-time status alone does not erase the end date.
- For non-student **USW staff-appointed term employees**, consecutive term employment cannot exceed **24 months**. If the term(s) exceed 24 months, the employee becomes a **continuing** or **sessional** employee, as applicable.
- For **USW casual employees**, there is a separate casual-to-staff-appointed conversion rule based on same-position hours and duration.

## Direct Answer To The Current Question
If you are a **full-time USW staff-appointed term employee** after one year and the employer agrees to continue you for a second year:

- You are not automatically continuing/permanent after only one year.
- You should expect written confirmation of the second year because the first term had a predetermined end date.
- If the employer continues the appointment so that non-student consecutive term employment goes **beyond 24 months**, the staff-appointed agreement says the employee becomes continuing or sessional, as the case may be.
- If your appointment letter already says **continuing** and has no end date, then you should not need a new annual contract for year two.

## Practical Checklist
- Check whether the appointment letter says **continuing**, **term**, **sessional**, or **casual**.
- If it says **term**, check the end date and ask HR/manager for written extension before the end date.
- Track consecutive term time. For non-students, the key USW staff-appointed threshold is **more than 24 months**.
- If casual, track same-position hours and duration against the 18-month / 24-month conversion rules.
