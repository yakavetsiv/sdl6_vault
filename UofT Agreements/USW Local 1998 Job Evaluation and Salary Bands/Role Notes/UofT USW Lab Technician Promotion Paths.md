# UofT USW Lab Technician Promotion Paths

> [!summary]
> Working note from a side conversation about U of T USW pay bands, Lab Technician roles, Hardware Technical Specialist, and restrictions on moving into higher-band roles. Verify against the current posting and formal job description before relying on a band/title match.

## Key Findings
- U of T USW has **20 numbered Pay Scale Groups / pay bands** in the public salary grid.
- The public HR site does **not** publish a complete public title-to-band map; the band is tied to the posted job and the USW Job Evaluation / Pay Equity Maintenance process.
- **Lab Technician / Laboratory Technician** postings vary by duties. Recent examples found in public postings include **Band 06** and **Band 09**.
- **Hardware Technical Specialist** postings in the Acceleration Consortium were found at **USW Pay Band 11**.
- The most direct same-ladder promotional target found was **Senior Laboratory Technician**, posted at **USW Pay Band 12**.

## Role Ladder Snapshot
| Role / direction | Band observed | Notes |
|---|---:|---|
| Lab Technician | 06 | Lower-band lab support postings, often term/grant-funded, technical execution and lab support. |
| Laboratory Technician / Chemical Stores Laboratory Technician | 09 | More responsible lab technician postings; may include chemical stores, safety, hazardous materials, equipment, and operational duties. |
| Hardware Technical Specialist | 11 | Engineering/technical specialization path; stronger fit for hardware, instrumentation, automation, 3D printing, CFD, coding, prototyping, and research equipment work. |
| CRAFT Technician / specialized facility technician | 11 | Specialized research infrastructure/core-facility technical path. |
| Senior Laboratory Technician | 12 | Cleanest direct promotion target from Lab Technician when responsibilities include lab operations, SOPs, safety training, troubleshooting, purchasing, data analysis, and research/funding support. |
| Lead Hand / technical operations roles | 14+ | Higher-responsibility technical leadership or operations path; usually requires broader scope or specialized trade/facility responsibility. |

## Restrictions and Conditions from Imported Agreement Notes
- **Probation first:** Under the USW appointed agreement, an employee must complete the probationary period before being eligible to apply and be considered for another position. The agreement lists probation as six months of active employment.
- **Promotion is competitive:** Internal applicants who meet minimum qualifications should be interviewed, but the successful candidate is selected as the demonstrably most qualified candidate. Seniority matters only when candidates are relatively equal.
- **External applicants can compete:** The hiring department can post externally and consider external applicants alongside internal employee applicants.
- **Ask for the job description:** Postings must indicate that a job description is available on request; the formal JD is important because pay band/classification depends on the evaluated duties, not only the title.
- **Reclassification is different from promotion:** Reclassification requires permanent and ongoing material changes to duties/responsibilities such that the job no longer falls within its current job class. A mere change in the mix or emphasis of existing duties is not enough.

## Practical Interpretation
For a current Lab Technician, the most practical promotional targets are:

1. **Band 09 Laboratory Technician** if currently in a Band 06 lab role.
2. **Band 11 Hardware Technical Specialist** if the record shows hardware, automation, device/prototype, instrumentation, coding, or advanced equipment work.
3. **Band 12 Senior Laboratory Technician** if the record shows independent lab operations, SOP/safety leadership, troubleshooting, purchasing authority, training, and research-output support.

For a reclassification case, collect evidence that the job permanently changed: new responsibilities, decision-making scope, equipment ownership, safety/compliance role, training duties, independent troubleshooting, purchasing/accountability, and changes to required skill/effort/responsibility/working conditions.
