## Comparison Finding
- The accepted edits changed the title toward **Research Coordinator** and added liaison, strategic initiative, literature/grant/report writing, event, consulting, administrative, confidentiality, proofreading, and diplomacy language.
- That mixed technical/admin direction did not create a clean higher-band case by itself.
- HR's likely view is understandable under U of T's USW framework: a title/JD wording change does not automatically change pay band.

## USW Rule Anchor
- U of T's USW job-evaluation memo says reclassification depends on **significant change**: permanent and ongoing added/removed/altered duties plus changed skill, effort, responsibility, or working conditions such that the job no longer falls within its current job class.
- A mere change in the mix or emphasis of duties is not enough.
- The Consistent JD/JDXpert material says the new JD format does not itself change pay band, classification, or responsibilities.

## Comparator Evidence
- **Senior Laboratory Technician, USW Pay Band 12** is the strongest comparator for a higher technical band.
  - Useful duty pattern: day-to-day lab operational needs, modifying experimental processes, funding/research-paper content, equipment troubleshooting and service-provider liaison, best-practice manual/SOPs, safety training/compliance, purchase approval, and non-supervisory job-related guidance.
  - Public posting: https://jobs.utoronto.ca/job/Toronto-Senior-Laboratory-Technician-ON/596017817/
- **Research Operations Officer, USW Pay Band 13** is a stretch comparator only if the role truly includes lab/research-unit operational oversight, workflow planning, communal equipment/resource scheduling, lab/facility commissioning/decommissioning, space/safety planning, project-cost/quote work, and service-delivery improvements.
  - Public mirror used in session: https://community.getro.com/companies/university-of-toronto/jobs/56894072-research-operations-officer
- **Research Project Manager, USW Pay Band 13** supports a research-management argument, but it is less aligned with a technical wet-lab reclassification argument unless the role genuinely includes project schedules, REB/grants, budgets, research assistant workflow supervision, stakeholder management, and complex research-data synthesis.

## Practical Decision
- Do not lead with **Research Coordinator** if the goal is a higher technical band; it can read as lower or mixed administrative work.
- Use **Research Technician** as a cleaner technical title if HR prefers it.
- Use **Senior Laboratory Technician** as the main comparator for the higher-band case, ideally phrased as a comparator/job-class review rather than only a title swap.
- The core argument should be: the permanent duties now match the higher-band technical pattern, not that the title itself requires a higher band.

## Blue DOCX Strategy
- The blue-text proposed DOCX adds substantial language around:
  - technical ownership of HOM-SDL organoid, organ-on-chip, imaging, automation, and AI-enabled workflows;
  - SOPs, sample/data traceability, quality-control checkpoints, and reproducibility;
  - independent troubleshooting of biological samples, imaging, automation, workstations, and equipment;
  - training/orienting students, staff, and collaborators;
  - safety/compliance responsibility;
  - purchasing/vendor/maintenance coordination;
  - research-output support;
  - consequences of error affecting samples, datasets, equipment, safety, project milestones, publications, and grants.

## Suggested Ask to HR
> Could we review this position against the **Senior Laboratory Technician** comparator/job class, rather than treating it as a generic Research Coordinator title change? The permanent duties now include senior technical lab operations, SOP ownership, safety training, specialized troubleshooting, research-output support, and organoid/automation workflow responsibility.
