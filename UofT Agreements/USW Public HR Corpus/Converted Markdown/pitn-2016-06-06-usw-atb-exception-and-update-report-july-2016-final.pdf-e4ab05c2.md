Overview

The USW ATB Exceptions and Update Report is a dynamic report up to the
date of central automation July 10th, 2016 and after that date it is a point in
time report. It is representative of data for a respective organizational unit.  It
is run by Business Officers to gain a listing of employees which will have their
ATB centrally processed and those which have exceptions and need to be
done manually by the department.  If the exceptions are corrected by July
10th, 2016 those employees will also be included in the central automation.
