# Pension Administration Transition Update – Member Questions and Information

From: [Ian MacEachern, Director, Benefits, Pension & Payroll](https://people.utoronto.ca/author/ian/)

As a result of the administration of the pension plan transitioning to the UPP Member Services team, we wanted to provide a resource to clarify UPP’s and U of T’s respective responsibilities on pension related topics and a reminder of pension information that is available on the HR Service Centre.

## **Where to Direct Employee Questions**

If employees have questions related to their pension, they are to be directed to UPP.  However, as there can be some confusion about the different retirement topics we thought the following may be helpful in outlining who is responsible for what when it comes to ‘pension’.

**UPP is responsible** for the following types of scenarios and individuals should be advised to contact UPP Member Services:

* Retirement estimates
* Completing pension paperwork
* Questions related to UPP entitlements
* Questions related to transferring pension entitlements into or out of UPP

To ensure personal information is not sent via regular email, UPP’s preferred methods of communication with plan members is via secure message within the [myUPP Member Portal](https://myupp.ca/members/member-portal/) or by phone, toll-free at 1-833-627-7877.

**U of T is responsible** for the following programs and information is available on the HR Service Centre:
