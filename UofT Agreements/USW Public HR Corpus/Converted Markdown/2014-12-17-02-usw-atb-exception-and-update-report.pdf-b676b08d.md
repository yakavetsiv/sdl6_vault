Overview

The USW ATB Exceptions and Update Report is a dynamic report up to the
date of central automation (January 9th at 5pm) and after that date it is a point
in time report. It is representative of data for a respective organizational unit.
It is run by Business Officers to gain a listing of employees which will have
their ATB centrally processed and those which have exceptions and need to
be done manually by the department.  If the exceptions are corrected by
January 9th, 2014 at 5 pm those employees will also be included in the
central automation.
