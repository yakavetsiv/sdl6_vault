# Retroactive July 1 ATB Increase Process for USW Local 1998 Appointed in HRIS

From: [Sheryl Vitug, Senior HRIS Training & Project Specialist](https://people.utoronto.ca/author/sherylv/)
To: Divisional HR Offices; Division of People Strategy, Equity & Culture; Business Officers via AMS listserv

Further to the USW [Local 1998 – Staff Appointed ratification memo](https://people.utoronto.ca/memos/agreement-ratified-between-university-and-usw-local-1998-staff-appointed-unit/) dated March 3, 2022, effective July 1, 2021, eligible USW Local 1998 – Staff Appointed are entitled to a retroactive 1% ATB increase based on their June 30, 2021 salary and must be processed in HRIS by **April 20** monthly pay close. Eligible USW include those who were actively employed as of the ratification date of March 2, 2022.

The[July 2021 Timeline for ATB Increase for USW](https://utoronto.sharepoint.com/%3Ab%3A/r/sites/psec-hris/HRIS%20Content/Timeline-for-July-2021-Retro-ATB-Increase-USW.pdf?csf=1&web=1&e=sjBfxD) and all resources listed below are available on the [HR Systems & Training Support Centre](https://utoronto.sharepoint.com/sites/hre-hris).

## **Reference Guide and Support**

The retroactive ATB increase will be automated centrally for USW excluding the employees with exceptions on their record. Departments will need to run the [USW ATB Exception and Update Report](https://utoronto.sharepoint.com/%3Ab%3A/r/sites/psec-hris/HRIS%20Content/USW-ATB-Exception-and-Update-Report-July-2021.pdf?csf=1&web=1&e=hIEuU1) and refer to the [USW Exception Descriptions and Corrective Action Reference Chart](https://utoronto.sharepoint.com/%3Ab%3A/r/sites/psec-hris/HRIS%20Content/USW-Exception-Descriptions-and-Corrective-Action-Reference-Chart-July-2021.pdf?csf=1&web=1&e=qcSq8e) for the appropriate transaction to be executed.

In addition, the following reports can be generated **before and after** processing the retroactive ATB increase:

* [Appointment Details Report – ZHSQAPPTDETL](https://utoronto.sharepoint.com/%3Ab%3A/r/sites/psec-hris/HRIS%20Content/Appointment-Detail-Report.pdf?csf=1&web=1&e=KNWsFh)
* [Basic Pay Report – ZHSQBASICPAY](https://utoronto.sharepoint.com/%3Ab%3A/r/sites/psec-hris/HRIS%20Content/Basic-Pay-Report.pdf?csf=1&web=1&e=0vtP8y)

This chart outlines the various scenarios and the corresponding processing details for those with exceptions.

| **Scenario** | **Processing Details and Procedure Documentation** | **Comments** |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Employee has July 1, 2021 segment  in Appointment Detail screen, with future or term end date and no segment after July 1, 2021. | [ATB-Increase-Process-for-USW-with-Segment-ON-July1-2021](https://utoronto.sharepoint.com/%3Ab%3A/r/sites/psec-hris/HRIS%20Content/Retro-ATB-Increase-Process-for-USW-with-Segment-ON-July1-2021.pdf?csf=1&web=1&e=n4ltT8) | This is also applicable for New Hire / Rehire effective July 1, 2021 |
| Employee has July 1, 2021 segment and one or more entries after  July 1, 2021 in Appointment Detail screen. | [ATB-Increase-Process-USW-with-Segments-AFTER-July1-2021](https://utoronto.sharepoint.com/%3Ab%3A/r/sites/psec-hris/HRIS%20Content/Retro-ATB-Increase-Process-USW-with-Segment-AFTER-July1-2021.pdf?csf=1&web=1&e=bG5OZI) | Examples include Change in FTE % or an Internal Org. Unit Change |
| Employee was hired /rehired after  July 1, 2021 with future or term end date. | [ATB-Increase-Process-USW-New Hire / Rehire-AFTER-July1-2021](https://utoronto.sharepoint.com/%3Ab%3A/r/sites/psec-hris/HRIS%20Content/Retro-ATB-Increase-Process-USW-New-Hire-Rehire-AFTER-July1-2021.pdf?csf=1&web=1&e=QB4huk) | Only for New Hire / Rehire effective **after** July 1, 2021 |

## **Questions and Contact Information**

Questions about eligibility should be directed to your [Divisional HR Office](http://contact.hrandequity.utoronto.ca/).

Please contact Central Payroll Services through the [HR Service Centre](http://uoft.me/hrsc) to update the Basic Pay for those employees receiving a Supplemental Unemployment Benefit (SUB) on July 1 for an approved leave of absence (e.g., Pregnancy, Parental, Adoption, Primary Caregiver Leave or Compassionate Family Caregiver Leave) by **April 19** payroll forms deadline.

If you need assistance with HRIS processing, please submit a ticket to HRIS Help Desk using the [HR Service Centre](http://www.uoft.me/hrisgethelp).
