# July 1, 2024 Salary Increase for Unionized Appointed and Non-Appointed Staff Group in HRIS

From: [The Division of People Strategy, Equity & Culture](https://people.utoronto.ca/author/hreadmin/)

Further to the recent [Memos of Agreement](https://people.utoronto.ca/agreements/) with USW Local 1998 Casual, CUPE 3261 Full-Time & Part-Time, CUPE 3261 Casual, CUPE 1230 Full-Time & Part-Time, and CUPE 1230 Student Casual, eligible staff in these groups are entitled to increases to their hourly / monthly rates effective July 1, 2024. These increases will be processed at the local level by **July 9 biweekly pay close** or **July 19 monthly pay close**.

|  |  |  |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **STAFF GROUP** | **PERCENT OF INCREASE or AMOUNT** | **ADDITIONAL INFORMATION** |
| [USW Casual](https://people.utoronto.ca/wp-content/uploads/2024/02/USW-CASUAL_MOA_20240216.pdf) | * Minimum rate of pay is $19/hour * Up to a 2% increase for those who are **not paid** at the minimum rate of pay and who are not paid in accordance with the salary grid for Staff-Appointed positions |  |
| [CUPE 3261 Full-Time & Part-Time](https://people.utoronto.ca/wp-content/uploads/2024/03/CUPE-3261-FTPT-MOA_.pdf) | * 2% increase based on June 30, 2024 wage rate | [Salary table](https://people.utoronto.ca/wp-content/uploads/2024/05/CUPE3261FT-PT-Temp-2023-25-no-formulas-240529.pdf) |
| [CUPE 3261 Temp](https://people.utoronto.ca/wp-content/uploads/2024/03/CUPE-3261-CASUAL-MOA.pdf) | * 2% increase based on June 30, 2024 wage rate | [Salary table](https://people.utoronto.ca/wp-content/uploads/2024/05/CUPE3261FT-PT-Temp-2023-25-no-formulas-240529.pdf) |
| [CUPE 3261 Casual](https://people.utoronto.ca/wp-content/uploads/2024/03/CUPE-3261-CASUAL-MOA.pdf) | * 2% increase based on June 30, 2024 wage rate   + Wage rates that are below $19/hour after the 2% increase has been applied shall be further increased to $19/hour | [Salary table](https://people.utoronto.ca/wp-content/uploads/2024/05/CUPE-3261-Casual-2023-2026-no-formulas-240530.pdf) |
| [CUPE 1230 Full-Time & Part-Time](https://people.utoronto.ca/wp-content/uploads/2024/04/MOA-CUPE-1230-Full-Time-and-Part-Time-NO-unpublished-LOIs_20240405-1.pdf) | * 2% increase based on June 30, 2024 wage rate | [Salary table](https://people.utoronto.ca/wp-content/uploads/2024/06/CUPE-1230-FT-PT-ranges-2024-2026.pdf) |
| [Student Casual 1230](https://people.utoronto.ca/wp-content/uploads/2024/04/MOA_CUPE-1230-Student-Casual_20240405-1.pdf) | * 2% increase based on June 30, 2024 wage rate | [Salary table](https://people.utoronto.ca/wp-content/uploads/2024/06/CUPE-1230-Student-Casual-2023-2026.pdf) |

## **Action Required:**

For all staff groups above ***except USW Casual***, the department would need to [Create Basic Pay](https://utoronto.sharepoint.com/%3Ab%3A/r/sites/psec-hris/HRIS%20Content/Create-Basic-Pay.pdf?csf=1&web=1&e=0ebBlZ&xsdata=MDV8MDJ8amVzcy54dUBVVE9ST05UTy5DQXw4OWZiMWYxZDlmNmI0M2E1ZWU1ODA4ZGM5NTFmN2VjYnw3OGFhYzIyNjJmMDM0YjRkOTAzN2I0NmQ1NmM1NTIxMHwwfDB8NjM4NTQ5MjA5NzAxMDM3NTgzfFVua25vd258VFdGcGJHWnNiM2Q4ZXlKV0lqb2lNQzR3TGpBd01EQWlMQ0pRSWpvaVYybHVNeklpTENKQlRpSTZJazFoYVd3aUxDSlhWQ0k2TW4wPXwwfHx8&sdata=VGVrZzZ1WDlhZkJRVDV5OE5YeVg1WUxzUmNCUzhWK24yNVlua045SVR5Yz0%3d) based on the new wage rates effective July 1, 2024. The [Basic Pay Report – ZHSQBASICPAY](https://utoronto.sharepoint.com/%3Ab%3A/r/sites/psec-hris/HRIS%20Content/Basic-Pay-Report.pdf?csf=1&web=1&e=0vtP8y&xsdata=MDV8MDJ8amVzcy54dUBVVE9ST05UTy5DQXw4OWZiMWYxZDlmNmI0M2E1ZWU1ODA4ZGM5NTFmN2VjYnw3OGFhYzIyNjJmMDM0YjRkOTAzN2I0NmQ1NmM1NTIxMHwwfDB8NjM4NTQ5MjA5NzAxMDU2ODMwfFVua25vd258VFdGcGJHWnNiM2Q4ZXlKV0lqb2lNQzR3TGpBd01EQWlMQ0pRSWpvaVYybHVNeklpTENKQlRpSTZJazFoYVd3aUxDSlhWQ0k2TW4wPXwwfHx8&sdata=TWlOd3NqMlkwSnJLSzUxQ05aalBwZ1AxYytHeTU3NFhKbnFrY1IrVEJaaz0%3d) must be generated after processing the salary increases to verify the required updates.

For USW Casual, the new wage rate would be used to process payment in Attendance (IT2002), Recurring Payments (IT0014) or Additional Payments (IT0015) infotypes effective July 1, 2024.
