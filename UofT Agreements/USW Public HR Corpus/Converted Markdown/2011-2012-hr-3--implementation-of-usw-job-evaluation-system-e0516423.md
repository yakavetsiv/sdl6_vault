# (2011-2012 HR#3) Implementation of USW Job Evaluation System

From: [The Division of People Strategy, Equity & Culture](https://people.utoronto.ca/author/hreadmin/)

|  |  |
| --- | --- |
| **Date:** | September 6, 2011 |
| **To:** | PDAD&C; Professional and Managerial Staff |
| **From:** | Angela Hildyard, Vice-President, Human Resources & Equity |
| **Re:** | **Implementation of USW Job Evaluation System** |

As of July 1, the new USW job classes and pay structure have been established and identified positions migrated to the new structure.

Processing the retroactive pay equity lump-sum payments for eligible employees is the next step of the project. These payments will be processed by September 28.

On September 12 all employees represented by USW local 1998 will receive a memo letting them know how the payments were calculated and when the payments can be expected. A copy of this memo will be sent to you once finalized.

For anyone with questions on this message or this process, please contact the appropriate Divisional Human Resources office. Resources and information on the USW Job Evaluation initiative, including links to recent memos, are available online at: <https://people.utoronto.ca/labourrelations/sesu.htm>.
