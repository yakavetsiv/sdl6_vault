# July 2022 Salary Increases for Appointed Faculty / Staff Groups – HRIS Processing Instructions

From: [Sheryl Vitug, Senior HRIS Training & Project Specialist](https://people.utoronto.ca/author/sherylv/)
To: Divisional HR Offices; Division of People Strategy, Equity & Culture; Business Officers

Further to the [Provost’s memo](https://memos.provost.utoronto.ca/agreement-on-merit-ptr-5-for-the-2021-22-academic-year-pdadc-30/) (PDAD&C #30), [2022 Compensation update for Confidential](https://people.utoronto.ca/memos/2022-compensation-update-for-confidential-pm-ap-and-ra-sra-staff/), [PM](https://people.utoronto.ca/memos/2022-compensation-update-for-confidential-pm-ap-and-ra-sra-staff/), [AP and RA / SRA staff](https://people.utoronto.ca/memos/2022-compensation-update-for-confidential-pm-ap-and-ra-sra-staff/), and [USW Local 1998 – Staff Appointed ratification memo](https://people.utoronto.ca/memos/agreement-ratified-between-university-and-usw-local-1998-staff-appointed-unit/), the following faculty and staff groups will have the corresponding salary increases effective July 1, 2022.

|  |  |
| --- | --- |
| **Faculty / Staff Group** | **Salary Increase** |
| Academic and Librarians | PTR / Merit Increase |
| PM (1-5), Confidential, Research Associate, Sr. Research Associate | 1% ATB increase based on June 30, 2022 salary  Merit Increase |
| PM (6-11) / Advancement Professionals | Merit Increase |
| Appointed USW | 1% ATB increase based on June 30, 2022 salary |

The salary increases must be processed in HRIS by **July 22** monthly pay close.

The following reports must be generated **before** to capture June 30, 2022 recorded data and **after** processing the salary increases based on July 1, 2022 – December 31, 9999 reporting period:

* [HR Data Verification Report](https://utoronto.sharepoint.com/%3Ab%3A/r/sites/psec-hris/HRIS%20Content/HR-Data-Verification-Report.pdf?csf=1&web=1&e=fhzj5E)
* [Appointment Details Report – ZHSQAPPTDETL](https://utoronto.sharepoint.com/%3Ab%3A/r/sites/psec-hris/HRIS%20Content/Appointment-Detail-Report.pdf?csf=1&web=1&e=KNWsFh)
* [Basic Pay Report – ZHSQBASICPAY](https://utoronto.sharepoint.com/%3Ab%3A/r/sites/psec-hris/HRIS%20Content/Basic-Pay-Report.pdf?csf=1&web=1&e=0vtP8y)
* [Absence Summary Report (Paid and Unpaid Long Term Absence)](https://utoronto.sharepoint.com/%3Ab%3A/r/sites/psec-hris/HRIS%20Content/Absence-Summary-Report%20.pdf?csf=1&web=1&e=csuS1Z)

It is important to note that any ***Appointment Changes, Progression on the Grid, Updates on CC/CFC*** effective July 1, 2022 and onwards should be processed **after** the salary increases have been done in order to minimize exceptions on the employee’s record.

### 2. USW

The ATB increase will be automated centrally on **July 8 at 5:00 pm** for USW staff, excluding those employees with exceptions on their record. Departments will need to run the [USW ATB Exception and Update Report](https://utoronto.sharepoint.com/%3Ab%3A/r/sites/psec-hris/HRIS%20Content/USW-ATB-Exception-and-Update-Report-July-2022.pdf?csf=1&web=1&e=vjDxfs) and refer to the [USW Exception Descriptions and Corrective Action Reference Chart](https://utoronto.sharepoint.com/%3Ab%3A/r/sites/psec-hris/HRIS%20Content/USW-Exception-Descriptions-and-Corrective-Action-Reference-Chart-July-2022.pdf?csf=1&web=1&e=TPb8GC)  for the appropriate transaction to be executed.

### Questions and Contact Information

Questions with respect to eligibility for Faculty and Librarian should be directed to your Dean / Chair’s Office.

Questions with respect to eligibility for Appointed Staff Group should be directed to your Divisional HR Office.

Please contact Central Payroll Services through the [HR Service Centre](http://uoft.me/hrsc), to update the Basic Pay IT0008 for those employees receiving a Supplemental Unemployment Benefit (SUB) on July 1 for an approved leave of absence, by **July 19** payroll forms submission deadline. Approved leave of absences include Pregnancy, Parental, Adoption, Primary Caregiver Leave or Compassionate Family Caregiver Leave.

If you need assistance with HRIS processing, please submit a ticket to HRIS Help Desk using the [HR Service Centre](http://www.uoft.me/hrisgethelp).
