# USW members ratify agreement with U of T

From: [The Division of People Strategy, Equity & Culture](https://people.utoronto.ca/author/hreadmin/)

|  |  |
| --- | --- |
| Date: | September 12, 2017 |
| To: | U of T Community |
| From: | Kelly Hannah-Moffat, Vice-President, Human Resources & Equity |
| CC: | Divisional HR Offices; The Bulletin Brief, The Provost’s Digest |
| Re: | Agreement ratified between University of Toronto and USW Local 1998 – Staff Appointed |

The University of Toronto (U of T) is pleased to announce that the tentative agreement reached September 3rd between the University and United Steelworkers (USW) Local 1998 (Appointed) has been ratified.

The Memorandum of Agreement will be posted under the [Agreements section](http://agreements.hrandequity.utoronto.ca/#USW1998_Appointed) on the Human Resources & Equity website.

Related U of T News article: [USW members ratify agreement with U of T](https://www.utoronto.ca/news/usw-members-ratify-agreement-u-t)
