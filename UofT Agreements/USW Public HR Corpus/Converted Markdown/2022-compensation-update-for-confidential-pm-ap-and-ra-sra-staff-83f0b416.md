# 2022 Compensation update for Confidential, PM, AP and RA / SRA staff

From: [The Division of People Strategy, Equity & Culture](https://people.utoronto.ca/author/hreadmin/); Jason Lum, Director, Compensation
To: Confidential Staff; Professional & Managerial Staff; Advancement Professionals; Research Associates & Senior Research Associates Cc: Divisional HR Offices

On March 17, 2021, Business Board approved a series of recommendations regarding compensation increases for Confidential, Professional & Managerial (PM), Advancement Professionals (AP), and Research Associate & Senior Research Associate (RA / SRA) staff.

**Merit increases**

The University administers annual merit increases for eligible non-represented staff groups in line with each group’s existing compensation policies.  Merit increases will be determined based on June 30, 2022 salaries and  implemented in the July 2022 pay.

**Across the Board (ATB) increase**

Across the Board (ATB) increases for eligible non-represented staff groups have historically been determined by taking into account the compensation increases negotiated with other unionized employee groups. In 2021-2022, compensation increases for non-represented staff (Confidential, Professional & Managerial (PM1-5) and Research Associates/Senior Research Associates) were consistent with negotiated increases for USW. Taking this into consideration, the recommended 1% ATB for Confidential, Professional & Managerial (PM 1-5) staff, Research Associates/Senior Research Associates (RA/ SRA) was approved for this year.

**An ATB of 1% effective July 1, 2022**

The 1% 2022 ATB increase will be effective July 1, 2022 based on June 30, 2022 salaries and implemented in the July pay.

In addition to maintaining consistency with unionized groups, the decision to provide an ATB to non-represented staff also ensures the University’s compliance with the compensation restrictions legislated under Bill 124.

**Reminders**

The deadline to complete and submit the activity report for each employee group is below. If you have any questions please contact: pmgm.help@utoronto.ca.

* April 21 for Professional Managerial, Confidential and Research or Senior Research Associates
* May 31 for Advancement Professionals

**Processing Instructions**

Detailed processing instructions for this year’s merit increases will be distributed to Divisional HR Offices and Business Officers in advance of the July pay close.

Should you have any questions, please contact your [Divisional HR Office](https://people.utoronto.ca/contact/).
