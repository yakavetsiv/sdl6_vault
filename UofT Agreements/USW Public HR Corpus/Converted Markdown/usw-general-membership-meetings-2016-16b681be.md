# USW General Membership Meetings 2016

By: [The Division of People Strategy, Equity & Culture](https://people.utoronto.ca/author/hreadmin/)

March 7, 2016

Members of United Steelworkers Local 1998 (appointed) group are advised that the Union has scheduled the following General Membership Meetings for 2016:

* **Tuesday April 12th 2016**
  Release time meeting, 3:30 p.m.
  Locations:
  + O.I.S.E., 252 Bloor Street West, Room 5170, University of Toronto St. George Campus
  + 1265 Military Road, Management Wing,MW229, University of Toronto Scarborough
  + Faculty Club, 3359 Mississauga Road N., DV3140, University of Toronto Mississauga

* **Thursday June 16th 2016**
  Release time meeting, 3:30 p.m.
  Location: TBD

* **Thursday December 15th 2016**
  Release time meeting, 3:30 p.m.
  Location: TBD

In accordance with the terms of the staff-appointed collective agreement between the United Steelworkers and the University of Toronto, “employees will be given time off (not to exceed two (2) hours), with no loss of regular straight time pay, up to three (3) times per year to attend General Membership Meetings of the Local Union”.

There is no comparable clause in the non-appointed (casual) collective agreement. However, such employees may attend on their own time if they are not scheduled to work.
