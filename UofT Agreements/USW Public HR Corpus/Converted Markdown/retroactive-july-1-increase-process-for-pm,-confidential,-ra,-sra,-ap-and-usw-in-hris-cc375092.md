# Retroactive July 1 Increase Process for PM, Confidential, RA, SRA, AP and USW in HRIS

From: [The Division of People Strategy, Equity & Culture](https://people.utoronto.ca/author/hreadmin/); Sheryl Vitug, Senior HRIS Training & Project Specialist
To: HR Divisional Offices; Division of HR & Equity; Business Officers

Further to the [Compensation Update for Confidential, PM, AP and RA/SRA](https://can01.safelinks.protection.outlook.com/?url=https%3A%2F%2Fhrandequity.utoronto.ca%2Fmemos%2Fcompensation-update-for-confidential-staff-professional-managerial-staff-research-associates-and-senior-research-associates%2F&data=04%7C01%7Calex.nathan%40UTORONTO.CA%7C246af3bdfe0d43258abe08d90ff9ba09%7C78aac2262f034b4d9037b46d56c55210%7C0%7C0%7C637558387548098591%7CUnknown%7CTWFpbGZsb3d8eyJWIjoiMC4wLjAwMDAiLCJQIjoiV2luMzIiLCJBTiI6Ik1haWwiLCJXVCI6Mn0%3D%7C1000&sdata=b1fhjfpMEtEmODBafvKGZtoC8eYT002wannvufMfwpA%3D&reserved=0) staff dated April 26, 2021 and [UofT News](%20https/www.utoronto.ca/news/u-t-provide-one-cent-increase-employees-affected-pandemic-related-compensation-freeze), effective July 1, 2020, the following eligible staff groups are entitled to a retroactive 1% ATB increase based on their June 30, 2020 salary and must be processed in HRIS by **May 19** monthly pay close.

* PM, Confidential, RA, SRA, and AP
* USW

The [July 2020 Timeline for Salary Increases for Appointed Staff Group](https://can01.safelinks.protection.outlook.com/ap/b-59584e83/?url=https%3A%2F%2Futoronto.sharepoint.com%2F%3Ab%3A%2Fr%2Fsites%2Fhre-hris%2FHRIS%2520Content%2FTimeline-for-July-2020-ATB-Increase.pdf%3Fcsf%3D1%26web%3D1%26e%3D32kzc3&data=04%7C01%7Calex.nathan%40UTORONTO.CA%7C246af3bdfe0d43258abe08d90ff9ba09%7C78aac2262f034b4d9037b46d56c55210%7C0%7C0%7C637558387548098591%7CUnknown%7CTWFpbGZsb3d8eyJWIjoiMC4wLjAwMDAiLCJQIjoiV2luMzIiLCJBTiI6Ik1haWwiLCJXVCI6Mn0%3D%7C1000&sdata=a83hdE1putfukSrVFVQbH7o8LHEr11da26yfvntC1x0%3D&reserved=0) and all resources listed below are available on the [HR Systems & Training Support Centre.](https://can01.safelinks.protection.outlook.com/?url=https%3A%2F%2Futoronto.sharepoint.com%2Fsites%2Fhre-hris&data=04%7C01%7Calex.nathan%40UTORONTO.CA%7C246af3bdfe0d43258abe08d90ff9ba09%7C78aac2262f034b4d9037b46d56c55210%7C0%7C0%7C637558387548108585%7CUnknown%7CTWFpbGZsb3d8eyJWIjoiMC4wLjAwMDAiLCJQIjoiV2luMzIiLCJBTiI6Ik1haWwiLCJXVCI6Mn0%3D%7C1000&sdata=WyrnitGWSIR4otpdlhrWZGmQkew9uQ1AJkPi0s6o1pI%3D&reserved=0)
