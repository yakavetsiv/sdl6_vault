# Benefit changes & Benefit plan premiums for the 2015 to 2016 plan year (United Steel Workers, Local 1998)

From: [The Division of People Strategy, Equity & Culture](https://people.utoronto.ca/author/hreadmin/)

|  |  |
| --- | --- |
| **Date:** | June 12, 2015 |
| **To:** | Colleagues who are members of CUPE Local 3261 (Full-time & Part-time) |
| **From:** | Steve Dyce, Director, Benefits & Pension, Division of Human Resources & Equity |
| **CC:** | HR Divisional Offices |
| **Re:** | **Benefit changes & Benefit plan premiums for the 2015 to 2016 plan year (CUPE Local 3261, Full-time & Part-time)** |

Changes recently negotiated to add coverage for naturopath, homeopath, acupuncture, osteopath, and occupational therapy under the Extended Healthcare (EHC) plan and an increase to the Vision Care (VC) plan maximum coverage will take effect July 1, 2015 as follows:

---

## Health & Vision Care Plans

---

### Extended Healthcare Plan:

The EHC plan existing coverage for chiropractor, physiotherapist, and registered massage therapist treatment will now include coverage for naturopathy (ND designation), homeopathy, acupuncture (acupuncturist of Ontario registration), osteopaths, and occupational therapy, under the same maximum $700 combined coverage per person per plan year.

As a result of this change, the full-time employee EHC monthly premium will **increase** from $17.36 to $17.48 (for single coverage), and from $63.10 to $63.56 (for family coverage).

### Vision Care Plan:

The Vision Care plan coverage maximum has been increased to $400 per covered person per 24 months.

As a result of this change, the full-time employee V C monthly premium will **increase** from $3.94 to $4.54 (for single coverage) and from $11.36 to $13.06 (for family coverage).

**As coverage is one month prepaid, these new premiums will take effect in your June 2015 pay.**

---

## Life Insurance

---

As result of claims experience, the life insurance premiums will **decrease** by approximately 15% for all employees effective July 1, 2015.

---

### For more information:

To review the current premium information for your benefits, [please visit the Employee Groups page](https://people.utoronto.ca/employee-groups/) of the HR & Equity website.

If you have any questions, please contact benefits.help@utoronto.ca.
