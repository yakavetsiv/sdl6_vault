# July 2024 Salary Increases for Appointed Faculty, Librarians, USW and Non-Represented Staff Groups – HRIS Processing Instructions

From: [Sheryl Vitug, Senior HRIS Training & Project Specialist](https://people.utoronto.ca/author/sherylv/)

Further to the [Provost’s memo](https://memos.provost.utoronto.ca/update-on-merit-ptr-5-for-the-2023-24-academic-year-pdadc-42/) (PDAD&C #42), [Compensation update for Confidential, PM, AP and RA / SRA staff memo](https://people.utoronto.ca/memos/2024-compensation-update-for-confidential-pm-ap-and-ra-sra-staff/), and [USW Local 1998 – Staff Appointed Memorandum of Agreement](https://people.utoronto.ca/wp-content/uploads/2023/09/USW-MOA_20230909.pdf), the following faculty, librarians, USW and non-represented staff groups will have the corresponding salary increases effective July 1, 2024.

|  |  |
| --- | --- |
| **FACULTY / STAFF GROUP** | **SALARY INCREASE** |
| Faculty and Librarians | * PTR / Merit Increase |
| PM (1-5), Confidential, Research Associate (RA), Sr. Research Associate (SRA) | * 2% ATB increase based on June 30, 2024 salary * Merit Increase |
| PM (6-11) / Advancement Professionals (AP) | * Merit Increase |
| Appointed USW | * 2% ATB increase based on June 30, 2024 salary |

The salary increases must be processed in HRIS by **July 19** monthly pay close.

The following reports must be generated **before** and **after** processing the salary increases based on July 1, 2024 – December 31, 9999 reporting period:

* [HR Data Verification Report as of July 1, 2024](https://utoronto.sharepoint.com/%3Ab%3A/r/sites/psec-hris/HRIS%20Content/HR-Data-Verification-Report.pdf?csf=1&web=1&e=fhzj5E)
* [Appointment Details Report – ZHSQAPPTDETL](https://utoronto.sharepoint.com/%3Ab%3A/r/sites/psec-hris/HRIS%20Content/Appointment-Detail-Report.pdf?csf=1&web=1&e=KNWsFh)
* [Basic Pay Report – ZHSQBASICPAY](https://utoronto.sharepoint.com/%3Ab%3A/r/sites/psec-hris/HRIS%20Content/Basic-Pay-Report.pdf?csf=1&web=1&e=0vtP8y)
* [Absence Summary Report (Paid and Unpaid Long Term Absence)](https://utoronto.sharepoint.com/%3Ab%3A/r/sites/psec-hris/HRIS%20Content/Absence-Summary-Report%20.pdf?csf=1&web=1&e=EXg3n5)

It is important to note that any **Appointment Changes, Progression on the Grid, Updates on CC/CFC** effective July 1, 2024 and onwards should be processed **AFTER** the salary increases have been done in order to minimize exceptions on the employee’s record.

## **Questions and Contact Information**

Questions with respect to eligibility for faculty members and librarians should be directed to your Dean / Chair’s Office.

Questions about eligibility for USW and non-represented staff groups should be directed to your [Divisional HR Office](https://people.utoronto.ca/contact/).

Please contact Central Payroll Services through the [HR Service Centre](http://uoft.me/hrsc), to update the Basic Pay IT0008 for those employees receiving a Supplemental Unemployment Benefit (SUB) on July 1 for an approved leave of absence, by **July 17** payroll forms submission deadline. Approved leave of absences include Pregnancy, Parental, Adoption, Primary Caregiver Leave or Compassionate Family Caregiver Leave.

If you need assistance with HRIS processing, please submit a ticket to HRIS Help Desk using the [HR Service Centre.](https://uoft.me/hrisgethelp)
