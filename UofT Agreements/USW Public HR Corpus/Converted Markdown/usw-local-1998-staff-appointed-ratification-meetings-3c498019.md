# USW Local 1998—Staff Appointed Ratification Meetings

From: [Kelly Hannah-Moffat, VP, People Strategy, Equity & Culture](https://people.utoronto.ca/author/khm/)
To: U of T Community

The United Steelworkers (USW) Local 1998 are holding Ratification Meetings for the renewal Staff-Appointed Collective Agreement as follows:

• Wednesday, September 13, from 3:30 pm to 5:00 pm
• Thursday, September 14, from 12:00 pm to 1:30 pm

USW Staff-Appointed employees need to be given release time to attend this meeting.
Employees are required to notify their manager if they are attending the meeting.
