# July 2015 ATB – USW and PM, Confidential, AP, RA and SRA Staff

By: [The Division of People Strategy, Equity & Culture](https://people.utoronto.ca/author/hreadmin/)

June 26, 2015

---

Below are the process details with respect to the July 1, 2015 automatic ATB increases for the above listed groups. We ask that you now run the Data Verification Report and Exception and Update Reports for your Organizational Units to view and correct the records with warnings and errors so those records with data integrity issues can be corrected and included in the automatic update taking place on **July 10th, 2015.**

Below are links to the timeline of the ATB Increase Process along with the Procedure and Support Documentation:

* [Timeline for July USW, PM, Conf., AP, RA and SRA ATB Increase Process](/wp-content/uploads/sites/31/2016/04/PITN-2015-06-26-01-Timeline-for-July-USW-PM-Conf-1.pdf "Timeline for July USW, PM, Conf., AP, RA and SRA ATB Increase Process")

**USW Documentation**

* [USW ATB Exception and Update Report](/wp-content/uploads/sites/31/2016/04/PITN-2015-06-26-02-USW-ATB-Exception-and-Update-Report-1.pdf "USW ATB Exception and Update Report")
* [Exception Descriptions & Corrective Action Chart](/wp-content/uploads/sites/31/2016/04/PITN-2015-06-26-03-Exception-Descriptions-and-Corrective-Action-Chart-1.pdf "Exception Descriptions and Corrective Action Chart")
* [ATB Increase Process for USW Staff with Segment Dates After July 1, 2015](/wp-content/uploads/sites/31/2016/04/PITN-2015-06-26-04-ATB-Increase-Process-for-USW-Staff-with-Segment-Dates-After-July-1-2015-1.pdf "ATB Increase Process for USW Staff with Segment Dates After July 1, 2015")
* [ATB and Progression on the Grid On July 1, 2015](/wp-content/uploads/sites/31/2016/04/PITN-2015-06-26-05-ATB-and-Progression-on-the-Grid-On-July-1-2015-1.pdf "ATB and Progression on the Grid On July 1, 2015")
* [ATB and Progression on the Grid After July 1, 2015](/wp-content/uploads/sites/31/2016/04/PITN-2015-06-26-06-ATB-and-Progression-on-the-Grid-After-July-1-2015-1.pdf "ATB and Progression on the Grid After July 1, 2015")

**PM, Conf., AP, RA and SRA Documentation**

* [PM, Confidential, AP, RA and SRA ATB Exception and Update Report](/wp-content/uploads/sites/31/2016/04/PITN-2015-06-26-07-PM-Confidential-AP-RA-and-SRA-ATB-Exception-and-Update-Report-1.pdf "PM, Confidential, AP, RA and SRA ATB Exception and Update Report")
* [Exception Descriptions & Corrective Action Chart](/wp-content/uploads/sites/31/2016/04/PITN-2015-06-26-08-Exception-Descriptions-and-Corrective-Action-Chart-1.pdf "Exception Descriptions and Corrective Action Chart")
* [ATB Increase Process for PM, Conf., AP, RA and SRA Staff with Segment Dates After July 1, 2015](/wp-content/uploads/sites/31/2016/04/PITN-2015-06-26-09-ATB-Increase-Process-for-PM-Conf-1.pdf "ATB Increase Process for PM, Conf., AP, RA and SRA Staff with Segment Dates After July 1, 2015")

You can run the respective ATB Update & Exception Reports throughout the entire month to manage the process.

* **Run it before July 10th, 2015**: to view the salaries for all USW, Confidential, PM, AP and RA / SRA Staff groups and easily identify what employees will processed automatically and which are required to be manually processed.
* **Run it after July 10th, 2015**: to view the updated salaries for all USW, Confidential, PM, AP and RA / SRA Staff groups and easily identify what employees you still need to process manually.

If you have any questions with respect to the processing of records in HRIS contact the HRIS Help Desk using the [AMS Form](http://sites.utoronto.ca/ams/help.htm "Help Form") or attend the get Help fast Session on the date listed in the Timeline document. Questions with respect to eligibility should be directed to your [Divisional HR office](https://people.utoronto.ca/resources/hristsc/hrpta/HRPITN/jatbusepmcaprasras.htm#div_off "HR Divisional Offices").
