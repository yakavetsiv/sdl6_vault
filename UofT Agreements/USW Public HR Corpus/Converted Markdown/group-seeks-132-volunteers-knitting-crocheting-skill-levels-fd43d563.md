# Group seeks 132 volunteers at all knitting / crocheting skill levels

By: [The Division of People Strategy, Equity & Culture](https://people.utoronto.ca/author/hreadmin/)

---

## Learn, network and help warm the campuses at U of T!

---

![Crochet needles and a ball of yarn](data:image/gif;base64...)

The USW 1998 Human Rights Committee is seeking 132 volunteers at all knitting and crocheting skill levels from the U of T community.

Starting September 19, groups will start work on creating 132 scarves, which will be displayed on trees around all three campuses on December 6, 2016.

Afterwards, these scarves will be distributed by USW Local 1998 members to those in need.

Each group will have one or more skilled instructor.

### Goal:

132 scarves will be created to recognize [Bill 132](http://www.ontla.on.ca/web/bills/bills_detail.do?locale=en&BillID=3535) — which amends various laws with respect to sexual violence, sexual harassment and domestic violence — for a one-day art installation of scarves across campuses on December 6th, 2016 to commemorate the École Polytechnique Massacre.

### Knitting Dates:

Monday, September 19, 2016
Monday, October 17, 2016
Monday, November 21, 2016

#### Time:

5:00 to 9:00 p.m.

#### St. George Campus Location:

25 Cecil Street

### Join Us:

To join, please email Tade (Teddy), Human Rights Committee Co-chair, with your skill level and location, or request where scarves may be dropped off if you are unable to knit in person.
