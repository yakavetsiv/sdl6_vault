# Self-Driving Laboratory Context

## Purpose In Paper

This cluster establishes why ExNode matters: SDLs are expanding, becoming distributed, and increasingly depend on reliable automation infrastructure.

## Core SDL Reviews

### `tom_self-driving_2024`

Broad Chemical Reviews article on SDLs for chemistry and materials science.

Use for:

- General SDL definition.
- Breadth of chemistry/materials applications.
- Framing SDLs as closed-loop systems.

ExNode angle:

- The review motivates the overall SDL stack, but ExNode focuses on the execution layer beneath planning and optimization.

### `tobias_autonomous_2025`

Review of SDL technology and policy implications.

Use for:

- Safety, security, societal, and workforce implications.
- Human accountability concerns.
- Cloud lab and remote experimentation context.

ExNode angle:

- Supports the argument that execution boundaries matter when AI-directed experimentation interacts with physical equipment.

### `hase_next-generation_2019`

Early framing of self-driving laboratories as AI-guided automated experimentation.

Use for:

- Historical motivation for AI plus automation.
- Closed-loop experimentation vision.

ExNode angle:

- Shows the long-standing emphasis on AI-guided experiment selection; ExNode complements this by making the execution side explicit.

### `martin_perspectives_2023`

Perspective on SDLs in synthetic biology.

Use for:

- Cross-domain relevance beyond chemistry/materials.
- Biological SDL motivations and constraints.

ExNode angle:

- Helps justify a device-agnostic execution abstraction useful across biology, chemistry, and materials science.

### `ishizuki_autonomous_2023`

Review of autonomous experimental systems in materials science.

Use for:

- Materials-science autonomous experimentation context.
- Human role in autonomous systems.

ExNode angle:

- Supports the human-in-the-loop/manual-control argument.

## Distributed and Accessible SDLs

### `baird_what_2022`

Minimal working example for SDLs.

Use for:

- Clarifying what minimal SDL components are.
- Arguing for small, reproducible automation units.

ExNode angle:

- ExNode can be presented as a minimal execution unit for the hardware side of an SDL.

### `strieth-kalthoff_delocalized_2024`

Distributed, asynchronous, closed-loop discovery across multiple laboratories.

Use for:

- Motivation for distributed orchestration.
- Multi-site coordination.

ExNode angle:

- ExNode provides local authority for each physical node in a distributed SDL.

### `canty_science_2025`

SDL accessibility, centralized facilities, and distributed networks.

Use for:

- Accessibility and collaborative infrastructure.
- Need for standardization across labs.

ExNode angle:

- Supports the claim that reusable local execution boundaries reduce the integration burden.

### `maffettone_what_2023`

Community perspective on gaps and challenges in autonomous discovery.

Use for:

- Open challenges in infrastructure, standards, education, and practice.

ExNode angle:

- Supports the problem statement around fragmented and hard-to-transfer SDL implementations.

## Suggested Manuscript Use

Use this cluster primarily in the Introduction. Keep it high level and do not overburden the architecture section with general SDL citations.
