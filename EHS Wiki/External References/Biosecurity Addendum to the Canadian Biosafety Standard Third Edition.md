## Date of coming into effect
The _Biosecurity Addendum to the Canadian Biosafety Standard, Third Edition_ comes into effect as a condition of risk group 4 pathogen and toxin licences and terrestrial animal pathogen import/transfer permits on January 5, 2026.
## Overview of the biosecurity addendum
The _Canadian Biosafety Standard, Third Edition_ (CBS) establishes requirements for the design, structure, and operation of containment zones to protect humans, animals, and the environment from the risks posed by certain pathogens and toxins. As an extension of the CBS, the _Biosecurity Addendum to the Canadian Biosafety Standard, Third Edition_ (the biosecurity addendum) establishes additional requirements for the highest containment facilities (i.e., containment level 4) to strengthen biosecurity oversight. The requirements found in the biosecurity addendum apply not only to the containment zone, but the premises in which the containment zone is housed.
Risk group 4 pathogen and toxin licence and terrestrial animal pathogen import/transfer permit holders must comply with applicable requirements set out in the CBS, including the biosecurity addendum, as a condition of their licence/permit.
Learn more:
  * [Canadian Biosafety Standard, Third Edition](https://www.canada.ca/en/public-health/services/canadian-biosafety-standards-guidelines/third-edition.html)

## Who the biosecurity addendum is for
  * Regulated facilities where risk group 4 regulated materials are handled or stored (i.e., containment level 4 facilities)
  * Risk group 4 pathogen and toxin licence and terrestrial animal pathogen import/transfer permit holders
  * Biological safety officers for containment level 4 facilities
  * Security personnel for containment level 4 facilities
