# Canadian Biosafety Standards and Guidelines

Source: https://www.canada.ca/en/public-health/services/canadian-biosafety-standards-guidelines.html

Summary: Landing page for Canadian biosafety standards, guidelines, directives, tools, and contacts.

## Converted Canada.ca Content

Safe handling of human and animal pathogens, toxins and plant pests in laboratories and containment zones.
## Services and information
### [Canadian Biosafety Standard, Third Edition](https://www.canada.ca/en/public-health/services/canadian-biosafety-standards-guidelines/third-edition.html)
Requirements for regulated laboratories and containment zones handling human or terrestrial animal pathogens and toxins.
### [Biosecurity Addendum to the Canadian Biosafety Standard, Third Edition](https://www.canada.ca/en/public-health/services/canadian-biosafety-standards-guidelines/biosecurity-addendum-canadian-biosafety-standard-third-edition.html)
Biosecurity requirements for regulated facilities where Risk Group 4 human or terrestrial animal pathogens are handled or stored.
### [Canadian Biosafety Handbook, Second Edition](https://www.canada.ca/en/public-health/services/canadian-biosafety-standards-guidelines/handbook-second-edition.html)
View the biosafety handbook on safe and secure handling of pathogens and toxins in Canada.
### [Canadian biosafety guidelines](https://www.canada.ca/en/public-health/services/canadian-biosafety-standards-guidelines/guidance.html)
Guides to biosafety plans, handling, best practices and more in Canada.
### [Biosafety directives and advisories](https://www.canada.ca/en/public-health/services/laboratory-biosafety-biosecurity/biosafety-directives-advisories-notifications.html)
Official communications about containment requirements for the safe handling of specific pathogens.
### [Canadian Biosafety App](https://www.canada.ca/en/public-health/services/canadian-biosafety-standards-guidelines/cbs-biosafety-app.html)
Use our free app to find specific requirements using Apple and Android devices.
### [Facilities handling aquatic animal pathogens](http://www.inspection.gc.ca/animals/aquatic-animals/imports/pathogens/facilities/eng/1377962925061/1377963021283)
Containment standards for facilities handling aquatic animal pathogens.
### [Facilities handling plant pests](http://www.inspection.gc.ca/plants/plant-pests-invasive-species/biocontainment/containment-standards/eng/1412353866032/1412354048442)
Standards for facilities handling plant pests.
### [Contact us](https://www.canada.ca/en/public-health/services/canadian-biosafety-standards-guidelines/contact-us.html)
How to contact us.
