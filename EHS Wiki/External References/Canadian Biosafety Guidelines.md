Summary: Guides to biosafety plans, handling, best practices, risk assessments, and reporting in Canada.

## Converted Canada.ca Content

As of March 26, 2026, the Public Health Agency of Canada amended the Human Pathogens and Toxins Act.
Learn more about the [new provisions coming into force immediately](https://www.canada.ca/en/public-health/services/laboratory-biosafety-biosecurity/human-pathogens-toxins-act.html).
An evergreen glossary is now available on the [PHAC Training Portal](https://training-formation.phac-aspc.gc.ca/course/view.php?id=1175&lang=en) and provides the most up-to-date definitions for terms used across Canadian Biosafety Guidelines. Individual glossaries will be removed from existing guidelines as they are updated.
