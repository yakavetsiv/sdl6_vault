# High Hazard Chemicals

Output page content
Work with the following chemicals requires that the Office of EHS be notified: high toxicity chemicals (LD50< 50mg/kg)

- high hazard corrosives (HF, aqua regia, piranha, perchloric, chromic etc.)
- confirmed and probable IARC Carcinogens (PDF)
- confirmed reproductive toxins/teratogens
- nanomaterials and
- when making novel compounds
