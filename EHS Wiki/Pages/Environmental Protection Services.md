### Environmental Protection Services Team & Contact Information

### Services

Environmental Protection Services, a division of Facilities & Services (F&S) is responsible for:

- Biological Waste Disposal
- Chemical Waste Disposal
- Radioactive Waste Disposal
- Ministry of Environment Generator Registration Information and Updates Waste Manifest Procedures
- Laboratory Closures (including Moving and Renovating)
- Bucket List Poster (PDF)
- PCB Disposal
- Gas Cylinders
- Battery Recycling Locations
- EPS Training Presentations
- responding to major Chemical Spill Emergencies
- Providing training, information and advice relating to storage, handling and disposal of chemical and radioactive wastes, incompatible chemical combinations and environmental legislation.
- Toxins and Treatments
- UTSC Biological Waste Procedures (PDF)
- Laboratory Hazardous Waste Management and Disposal Manual

*See Environmental Protection Services mention in the University of Toronto 2017-18 Sustainability Yearbook
