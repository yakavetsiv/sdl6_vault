# X-ray Safety

Output page content
The University of Toronto X-ray Safety Program is governed by the Institutional Radiation Protection Committee (IRPC), which is responsible for reviewing and approving the institutional X-ray Safety Program and ensures that all components of the program are adequately implemented. The Radiation Protection Service (RPS) under the office of Environmental Health and Safety (EHS), has the responsibility of implementing, updating, and auditing the X-ray Safety Program in compliance with the Occupational Health and Safety Act, R.R.O. 1990, Regulation 861 (PDF)
