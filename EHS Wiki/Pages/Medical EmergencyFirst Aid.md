# Medical Emergency/First Aid

Output page content
Updated: January 23, 2024

1. Immediately provide first aid / medical aid (doctor, hospital) if necessary. In the event of a medical emergency, always call (9) 9-1-1.
2. Contact Campus Safety after you have called 9-1-1, or direct another person to contact them while you call 9-1-1. Refer to https://www.utoronto.ca/campus-status . UTSG / UTSC Campus Safety: 416-978-2222 UTM Campus Safety: 905-569-4333
3. Provide and pay for immediate transportation to a hospital, health professional office/clinic, or the worker’s home (if necessary).
4. Report the incident to your supervisor/academic contact immediately. The supervisor / UofT contact must complete an Online Accident/Incident eForm within 24 hours.
5. Investigate the incident. For Critical Injuries , follow the same steps above. In addition, call Campus Safety, secure the scene, and do not disturb the scene. Additional reporting requirements apply and will be facilitated by EHS ( ehs.office@utoronto.ca ; 416-978-4467).
