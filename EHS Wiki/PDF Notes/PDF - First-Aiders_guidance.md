Definitions:

First Aiders Guidance

First Aid: Immediate care given to a person who is injured or ill. Treatment is given until
medical aid by a health care professional can be administered (if applicable).

First Aid Box: A box or container that must, at the minimum, contain items listed in: Sections 8,
9, 10, 11 or 16 of Regulation 1101; or, the CSA Standard, First Aid Kits for the Workplace
Z1220-17

First Aider: A person holding a valid First Aid Certificate issued by a training agency
recognized by the Workplace Safety & Insurance Board (WSIB).

First Aider Responsibilities:

1.  To respond to first aid emergencies within the limits of their training.

2.  To obtain and maintain a valid First Aid Certificate issued by a training agency

recognized by the WSIB.

3.  To follow appropriate emergency procedures and provide interim first aid until medical

services takes over.

4.  To be in charge of a first aid station and inspect the first aid box and its contents
at least every three months (4X/year) and replenish the supplies if needed. The
inspection card/checklist for the first aid box should be signed and dated. Refer to this
link for a checklist: https://ehs.utoronto.ca/training/first-aid-training/#checklist

5.  To keep a record, indicating the date, time, and nature of the first aid treatment given to

any injured person.

Advise injured worker to complete a University of Toronto accident / incident online
eForm report with their supervisor within 24 hours. Details and information on how to
complete this form is located on the EHS website at: https://ehs.utoronto.ca/report-an-
incident/ .

First Aid Stations shall contain/post:

A first aid box* containing the
items required by Reg. 1101 or
CSA
the WSIB Form 82 poster,
‘In Case of Injury at Work’**, and

an inspection card/checklist,

valid first aid certificates of the
first aiders.

Questions? Contact EHS at Tel: 416.978-4467, email: ehs.office@utoronto.ca

Or visit the First Aid Training website: https://ehs.utoronto.ca/training/first-aid-training/

Other personal protective equipment (e.g., CPR barrier mask and non-latex gloves) as prescribed by the
first aid training should also be included in the first aid box.
*You can purchase ready-made first aid kits from the supplier of your choice, or you may assemble
your own first aid kit following appropriate content requirements.
**Contact EHS for the ‘In Case of Injury at Work’ WSIB Poster / sticker or go to this link:
https://www.wsib.ca/en/case-injury-poster-form-82 to download a poster.
