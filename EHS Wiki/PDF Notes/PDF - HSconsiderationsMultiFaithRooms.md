Health & Safety Considerations for the Management of a Multi-Faith
Room

1.  Ensuring a clean and well-maintained space.

a.  Post a procedure for reporting a problem about the room / facility, including a

contact email and/or telephone number of person responsible and the relevant
Facilities number (St. George campus call 416-978-3000, Mississauga campus
submit work order in ServiceNow: https://uoft.service-now.com/utm, Scarborough
campus contact: https://www.utsc.utoronto.ca/facmgmt/). Concerns may include:

i.  Cleanliness
ii.  Damage
iii.  Temperature
iv.  Lighting
v.  Misuse of the room

Concerns regarding other users may be reported to the local Equity, Diversity
and Inclusion (EDI) lead or the Institutional Equity Office.

Urgent concerns

b.  In case of fire or emergency evacuation, follow procedures in the room. Room

should be posted with building address, and room number. Consult your Fire
Prevention Officer https://www.fs.utoronto.ca/contact/fire-prevention-officer/ (St.
George) or local campus facilities team where applicable.
In the event of incident or concern that requires attention, contact Campus Safety
by calling 416.978-2222 (all three campuses). Link to Campus Safety contacts
for each campus:  https://www.utoronto.ca/campus-status

c.

d.  For emergencies that require an immediate response, call 9-1-1 and then notify

Campus Safety.

e.  Report incidents or injuries to the UofT contact who will complete an
Accident/Incident e-Form: www.ehs.utoronto.ca/report-an-incident

4.  Provide appropriate storage for personal items such as shoes.
5.  Review security needs based on the hours of operation and access after-hours,

weekends, holidays.

6.  Post emergency exit procedures, safety information (e.g. “No Smoking”, “Fire Exit”, “No

Flames”) in visible areas and the EHS incident reporting procedure decal. Safety
information should not be covered or removed by users.

7.  Posting Expectations or Community Guidelines. Consider including:

•  Please reset the room and leave the space tidy for the next user. Please wipe
down all mats, benches or other materials after use and return them to the
appropriate cabinets or bins. Remove any materials for disposal before
departing.

•  Please be mindful of noise levels when engaging in spiritual or reflective practice.

Multi-faith spaces are often located near other areas used for work, study,
teaching, learning, and research.

•  With the exception of drinking water, food and beverages are generally not

permitted in the room.  With regard to spiritual practices involving food and drink,
please check with space administrators to determine where this may be allowed.
For instance, there are certain spaces within the Multi-Faith Centre on the St.
George campus which do allow for these practices to take place.

•  Where applicable, no candles, incense, or open flames of any kind. (Per above,

this does not apply to Indigenous Ceremonial Practices and locations where such
activities are permitted.)

•  Posters and notices may not be posted in the room. Instead, consider a bulletin
board at the entrance to the room only, where notices related to religious,
spiritual, and reflective practice may be posted. Notices will be removed every
two to three weeks

Infrastructure Considerations

For departments that are considering using a space for spiritual or reﬂective practice here are some
considerations:

Location and orientation. The room should be away from the noise and vibration (e.g. mechanical
room) or high traffic areas (e.g. main entrance, lecture rooms, etc.). Privacy and accessibility are the
key for the location. The room should be positioned on the following with proximity to multiple exits,
preferably in different directions (e.g. the middle hallway with the option to exit to the opposite
direction if one direction is blocked).  Avoid the terminal ends of the hallways in the basement,
where the escape routes can be limited.

Ventilation. The room should be well-ventilated and should consider the purpose of the room, the
expected activities, and the number of expected users. In a situation where the room can be used
for smoke, perfume/scented oils, or smoke (tobacco, sage, etc.), the ventilation should be isolated
from the rest of the building and designed with 100% exhaust without recirculation. Please consult
with Fire Prevention (St. George) or the local facilities team.

Ablution (washing) station. The location shall be near the prayer room with a clear path between.
Ensure non-slip ﬂooring or non-slip mats, grab bars for accessibility and thermostatically controlled
water to prevent scalding. Consider a sensor-activated/hands-free faucet.

Changes to the space: When required, engage Facilities and Services to install items (markers,
shelves, alters, etc.) that require drilling/cutting the building fabric. Provide information to users on
who to contact to report a problem or to make changes to the space.

Soundprooﬁng. Consider the types of noise-generating activities that may occur (e.g. music,
drums, vocal prayer, etc.) in this room and consider another location where applicable. Where
another location is not feasible, departments may need to consider engaging a noise consultant to
provide advice on noise reduction strategies (e.g. noise-canceling materials). Note: noise can
penetrate not only walls but also be transmitted from large glass panels and transferred through the
ductwork and air ventilation grills.

Security. Consider the hours of use (e.g. evenings, weekends) and consult with Campus Safety as
needed (e.g. “blue button” intercom, emergency assistance summoning buttons, cameras, etc.).
Post emergency contact information (e.g. Campus Safety emergency phone number 416-978-2222)
and resources such as Working Alone, Travel Safer, Awareness Posters, etc.
