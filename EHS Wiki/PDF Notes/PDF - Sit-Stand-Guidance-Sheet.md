Sit-Stand Desks

A sit-stand desk allows the user to alternate between sitting
and  standing  by  being  able  to  lower  or  raise  the  desk.
Having  an  option  to  work  from  a  fully  seated  or  fully
standing  position  provides  an  opportunity  for  postural
change.

There are three main types of sit-stand desks:
• Mechanical (manual)     • Standing desk converter
• Electrical

When working at a sit-stand workstation, proper ergonomic
set  up  is  very  important.  It  is  essential  to  make  sure  the
workstation  meets  the  needs  of  the  user  when  both
standing and sitting.

How long should a person sit or stand?
Sit/stand times can vary for each person.

As a general guideline,
for every 30 minutes:

• Sit for 20 minutes
• Stand for 8 minutes
• Stretch/move for 2 minutes
