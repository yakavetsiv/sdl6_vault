Student Placement & Co-op Checklist

Under the Ontario Occupational Health and Safety Act (OHSA), a worker is also defined as “a person
who performs work or supplies services for no monetary compensation under a program approved by a
college of applied arts and technology, university, career college or other post-secondary institution.”
Therefore, students on UofT premises completing a student placement must receive training and
instructions equivalent to that of a UofT employee.

This Checklist provides guidance to departments on the types of information to include in an
orientation/onboarding process. Signatures at the bottom of the checklists demonstrates
acknowledgement, acceptance and understanding of their obligations and responsibilities. Departments
are to maintain copies of the completed checklist.

Departments that receive UofT or external students for placement opportunities (“placement
employer”): the Part 1 checklist may be used to document health and safety orientation provided to a
student(s) prior to exposure to any hazards in your workplace.

Departments that send UofT students to external placements: UofT students must follow training,
procedures and processes at their placement site. A template (Part 2) is available for UofT departments
to use if they wish to develop a checklist. Departments can also use the template to keep track of
general onboarding items such as confirmation of keys, access, building site tour etc., to vet the external
placement employer.

Co-operative education (co-op): Co-op integrates academic study terms with paid periods of relevant
work experience. A student on a co-op is a worker of the co-op employer. As such, the external co-op
employer or partner is responsible for ensuring all relevant workplace legislation is followed when the
student is performing work activities on behalf of the co-op employer. A template (Part 2) is available for
UofT departments to develop a checklist to gather health and safety information about a prospective co-
op employer.

UofT Departments that have/receive health and safety concerns about placement and co-op employers
are encouraged to use the checklist template in Part 2 as a guide to determine whether the placement or
co-op should continue. Departments are also encouraged to contact EHS (ehs.office@utoronto.ca) for
assistance.

If you have any questions or require additional support, please contact EHS (ehs.office@utoronto.ca).
For additional information about Off-Campus Safety, please refer to the EHS Off-Campus Safety page.
