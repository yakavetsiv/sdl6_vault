Don’ts

To solve common ergonomic problems
and adjust your workstation to ﬁt you.

Don’t bend your wrists when typing/mousing.

Wrists should be neutral and should not bend
up, down, or to the sides.

Take regular activity breaks.

Vary your tasks (e.g., photocopy) and take visual breaks.
Focus on a distant object for 10-15 seconds every
20 minutes on the computer.

Don’t twist your neck or body to view your
monitor or use the keyboard.

Keep your body, keyboard, and monitor in a straight line.

Use blinds and curtains to reduce solar glare.

Don’t tuck your legs under your chair.

Also adjust the tilt of your monitor to
avoid glare from overhead light ﬁxtures.
