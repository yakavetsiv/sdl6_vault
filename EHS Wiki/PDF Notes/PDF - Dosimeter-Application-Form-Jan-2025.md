Application for Personal Dosimeter
Radiation Protection Service

The  Health  Canada  National  Dosimetry  Service  (NDS)  maintains  permanent  records  of  personal
lifetime  doses.  The  information  in  the  National  Dose  Registry  is  recorded  based  on  your  Social
Insurance Number, last name, given name, date of birth, province/country of birth, and job title.

The  University  of  Toronto  and  the  National  Dosimetry  Service  are  committed  to  respecting  the
personal  privacy  of  people  who  require  personal  lifelong  occupational  monitoring  for  ionizing
radiation. Any information collected is disclosed only to National Dosimetry Service employees and
other officials who need it to process records to update the National Dose Registry, according to any
of the legislation by which the National Dosimetry Service is governed. The University of Toronto and
the National Dosimetry Service does not create individual profiles from the information that radiation
users provide. The University does not disclose or provide personal information to any party inside or
outside the federal government unless authorized by law.
