HECHMET - VIM

• HECHMET (Higher Education Cooperative for

Hazardous Material and Equipment Tracking) is a
consortium of Canadian universities using Vertére
Inventory Management (VIM) software system.

• Access from any computer connecting to U of T

network (includes VPN access):
https://hechmet.vimenterprise.com/uot

• Contact

Email: hechmet@utoronto.ca
Phone: 416-978-6641

What Items are required in
Inventory?

• Original chemical containers with the following

WHMIS pictograms are required to be in HECHMET,
with a small quantity exemption: 5g (5mL) or less
without an Acute Toxic pictogram. For chemical
containers received before December of 2018, refer
to Safety Data Sheets.

3

Implementation

• EHS Provides:

– User Guide, Training, Group/User/Location setup and update.
– Pre-printed barcode labels.

• Groups can Specify in Discussion with EHS:

– Group/user/location tree setup.
– A list of rooms storing hazardous chemicals and whether they are

shared with other PI(s).

– Optional sub-locations (e.g., flammable cabinet, acid cabinet,

Chemical shelf, fridge etc.)

• Hardware:

–

Barcode scanner (~$50, PI or Department)
Recommend links: scanner 1, scanner 2 with the additional feature
of reading barcodes from screens.

Ongoing Inventory Management

• Recommendations to PIs

– Ensure all hazardous chemicals are in your HECHMET

inventory.

– Add newly received chemicals to HECHMET asap.
– Remove the barcode labels from empty containers
and scan to “Dispose” in HECHMET periodically.
– Conduct Physical Inventory annually (may use a

barcode scanner).

– Provide training to all new lab members.
– Contact EHS if you are moving labs.

Chemical Inventory – Add: Inventory
Catalog Search

First, perform a Search to find the chemical in system’s shared
Catalog used by HECHMET consortium.

Use either the Product No. or the CAS # in your initial search. Repeat search if item not found
by using the other product identifier. Stock number is a size-specific product number. Vendor
is defined as the name displayed on the chemical container. See next page for an example. If
no record is found, please send an email to hechmet@utoronto.ca, EHS staff will add the
chemical to shared catalog for you. PIs and lab managers are not able to access Catalog Add.

Chemical Inventory – Add: Tips for affixing a
barcode label on a chemical container
• For optimal barcode scanner readability, affix
the HECHMET barcode label on a small flat
area (~1.5”x0.5”, either horizontally or
vertically) on the container.

• To prevent barcode labels from falling off from
the chemical containers, ensure the surface
where the barcode will be affixed is clean &
dry, especially containers stored in fridges and
freezers.

Chemical Inventory – Management

The chemical inventory must be updated and well maintained.
Principal Investigators (PIs) and Lab/Store Managers have the
rights to update the inventory in their group, which include
edit, transfer, dispose, change tags, update consumption etc.

43

Chemical Inventory - Management:
Transfer

To transfer a chemical, search for it using the Barcode #
or other criteria. Add a check mark beside of the
chemical and click the Transfer button. You can transfer
a chemical to another location/PI/Group.

Chemical Inventory - Management:
Transfer

Select the Location, PI and Group that the chemical will
be transferred, click Complete Transfer button. Then click
Search to return the main view/update search page.

Chemical Inventory - Management:
Dispose

To dispose of a chemical, click in the Barcode #
field and scan the HECHMET barcode. Select the
chemical by placing a check mark on the left side
of the pencil icon. Click Dispose button. To dispose
of multiple chemicals at the same time, continue
to scan HECHMET barcodes in the Barcode # field,
then place a check mark on “Select all on page”,
click Dispose button.
