WEAR:

• Safety glasses (CSA or ANSI-certiﬁed) to protect your eyes in case

the blade breaks.

• Cut or puncture-resistant gloves (e.g., box cutter gloves) to prevent

injury (e.g., from utility knife, sharp wires).

Utility Knives

INSPECT:

• The tool for defects before and after use. Take defective tools out of

service.

• The blade to ensure it is sharp and properly positioned in the handle.

Follow manufacturer’s instructions for changing blades.

X-Acto Knives

WHEN USING TOOL:

• Use tools only as intended and follow any manufacturer instructions.

• Choose tools that have safety handles. Hold tool by the handle

and with your dominant hand.

• Do not apply excessive pressure on the blade or rush during cutting.

• Retract/close blades fully and place guard back in place after use.

Paper Cutters

• Rest material to be cut on a stable surface. Use a cutting board

Tape Dispensers

and/or slip-resistant matting to prevent sliding. Ensure material is
properly secured before cutting.

• Cut away from your body. Keep extremities and body out of cutting

path. Work in a well-lit space.

• For any replacement parts (e.g., blades), ensure they are sealed in a

hard container or original packaging. Dispose of dull or broken
blades in a puncture-resistant container.

• Consider tools with a self-retracting mechanism, shielded blade, or

rounded tip blade to reduce the risk of injury.

• Do not pass or throw a tool to someone else. Carry tool with pointed
end facing down. Place tool on counter and allow other person to
pick it up.

Scissors

• Store tools properly. Avoiding leaving out on tables or workspaces

after use.
