Board of Laser Safety®

Providing a means for improvement in the practice of laser safety

March 27, 2017

Dr. Sandu Sonoc
University of Toronto, Environmental Health and Safety
215 Huron Street, 7th Floor
Toronto, ON  M5S 1A2  Canada

Dear Sandu,

This letter is to inform you of the status of your request for BLS Certification
Maintenance (CM) points. The material you submitted has been reviewed and will be
awarded CM points to CLSOs and/or CMLSOs as follows:

Sponsoring Organization:  University of Toronto, EHS

Activity:  Laser Safety Officer Training

CM Points Awarded:  4 total (1 point per each day attended)

BLS CM Number:  106-3839

CM points are accrued by Certified Laser Safety Officers (CLSOs) and Certified Medical
Laser Safety Officers (CMLSOs) who are certified by the Board of Laser Safety (BLS).
Verification of the activity must include the following: a certificate, letter, attendance
roster or some other documentation from the sponsoring organization that validates
attendance.

Sponsoring organizations are asked to distribute verification documentation directly to
the CLSO and/or CMLSO if possible. Please inform CLSOs and CMLSOs who attend
your activity the number of BLS CM points that were awarded and the BLS CM number
assigned.

Please do not hesitate to contact me if you have any questions or need further
assistance.

Sincerely,
