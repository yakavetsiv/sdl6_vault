Should any of these items fail the
inspection, do not use, tag ladder
and take out of service, and
report to a person in charge.
Frame, Rails, Rungs and Steps:
□ Not loose, cracked, bending, or missing.
□ No sharp edges.
Non-Skid Feet, Castors and Lock:
□ Casters retract under the operator’s
weight.
□ Locking mechanism correctly catches lock
and unlocks.
□ Non-slip rubber feet are not worn, missing
or damaged.
Rivets, Screws, Bolts, Nuts and Other Metal
Parts:
□ Not loose or missing.
General:
□ No signs of damage (e.g., from rust,
heat/fire, or corrosive substances).
□ Free from slippery substances (e.g., water,
grease, wet paint), dirt and/or debris.
□ Free from signs of temporary repair or
unauthorized modification.
□ Stable, level and not wobbly.
□ All manufacturer stickers and markings
clear and visible.
□ Inspection tag is present and checked.
WARNING!
If ladder is damaged or missing parts, the ladder
must be taken out of service immediately.
Metal ladders must not be used in areas where
there is a risk of electrical contact.
