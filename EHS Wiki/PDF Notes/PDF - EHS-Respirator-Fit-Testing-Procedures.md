EHS Respirator Fit Testing Procedures

1.  EHS follows applicable hygiene practices, such as frequent hand washing and wearing of

disposable nitrile gloves, while handling fit test equipment and respirators. Fit test
equipment will be wiped down with disinfecting wipes before the session and between
each fit test participant. Hand sanitizer is available for use.

2.  In general, only the fit tester and the employee/student being tested will be allowed in the
room at one time. For larger rooms, individuals waiting to be fit tested can wait in the room
if they choose to do so. The employee/student being fit tested will sit at a designated seat
during the assessment.

3.  The fit tester will install the tubing to the probe (for N95 respirators) or attach respirator

cartridges with tubing connected (for half- or full-face masks) onto the respirator that the
employee/student brings with them to the fit test.

4.  Employee/student are expected to bring their own respirator (NIOSH-approved or CSA-

certified N95 or elastomeric half-face/full-face mask). For elastomeric respirators, P100
respirator filter cartridges will be provided by EHS for fit testing purposes.

5.  If the employee/student does not bring their own N95 and/or elastomeric respirator to the

fit test, the fit tester will fit the individual on a default model(s) provided by EHS.

6.  The employee/student will be given instructions on how to don the respirator. A mirror to

assist with wearing the respirator may be provided.

7.  The fit test assessment consists of 7 breathing exercises guided by the software. During this
assessment, the fit tester will monitor and provide instructions to the employee/student.
8.  After the fit test is complete, and with the employee/student still wearing the respirator, the
fit tester will demonstrate how to properly perform a positive and negative pressure seal
check (see Resources section below for additional guidance).

9.  The employee/student will then doff the respirator and pass the respirator back to the fit
tester. The fit tester will remove the tubing and/or respirator cartridges (if applicable).
10. N95 respirators which have been used for the fit test will be disposed. For half- or full-face

respirators, the fit tester will demonstrate how to properly disinfect the elastomeric
respirator and respirator cartridges using a respirator cleaning wipe.

11. A fit test wallet-size certificate card will be given to the staff/student upon successful

completion of the fit test.

12. Reminders – employee/student must be clean shaven, have taken the online training, and
provide their completed respirator user screening form prior to their fit test. Individuals
should present themselves for fit testing in the same personal condition they expect to be
in when using the respirator, including any personal protective equipment (PPE) and
personal effects/accessories. This includes hearing, face/eye, and head protection, hair
styles (e.g., ponytails), head coverings, and wearing of dentures/braces/aligners (e.g.,
Invisalign), prescription glasses, or contact lenses. EHS has a few models of PPE available
(i.e., safety goggles and glasses, overhead earmuffs) if the employee/student requests
them for the purpose of the fit-testing, but best practice is for the employee/student to
bring the actual models they use during the activity.
