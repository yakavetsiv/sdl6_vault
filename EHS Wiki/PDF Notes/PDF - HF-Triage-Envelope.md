HYDROFLUORIC ACID (HF)

General Info

•  HF is a very toxic and corrosive acid
•  HF quickly penetrates tissues resulting in burns that require specialized treatment
•  Cardiac symptoms can be delayed up to 24 hours after skin exposures
•  Exposure of less than 10% of the body to HF can be fatal
•
•  Can cause hypocalcemia, cardiac arrythmias, metabolic imbalances, pulmonary edema
•  Patient requires ECG and cardiac monitor

In high concentration can lead to systemic toxicity

Caution: Wear nitrile gloves when touching the patient or removing their clothing!
