DON’T LET THE
BED BUGS BITE

It’s possible to pick up bed bugs almost any place. They can
hitch a ride home with you in your luggage, your personal
belongings or even your furniture — and once they’re in your
home, they can be extremely difficult to get rid of.

Here’s what you need to know, look for and do to catch bed
bugs early and help reduce your chances of a costly infestation.

Did A Bed Bug Bite?

Whether bite reactions occur
immediately or take a few
days to show symptoms, the
following factors may help you
determine if you’ve been bitten.

Bites are commonly found on body parts
that are unconstricted during sleep (face,
neck, arms, legs, hands and shoulders).

Bites may appear in tight groups of
small, red marks.

Bites may become inflamed, itchy or
even blistered, especially if irritated.
