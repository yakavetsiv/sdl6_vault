# Acceleration Consortium — Image Style Guide

## Three Image Types

AC uses exactly three image categories. Every image in an AC presentation must belong to one.

| Type | Purpose | Frequency |
|------|---------|-----------|
| **Abstract** | Inspire imagination about materials/future | Sparingly — title slides, section dividers |
| **Photography** | Show real science, equipment, application | Primary content imagery |
| **Diversity** | Show real people, community, inclusion | People slides, about/team sections |

---

## Type 1 — Abstract

### What It Looks Like (from brand guide frames)

- **Subject:** Physical objects — crystals, gemstones, faceted geometric forms, translucent mineral structures, close-up material surfaces
- **Background:** Pure black or very dark
- **Colour:** Dominated by warm orange-red (`#FF3D00` range) + amber-gold (`#F7B833`), with cool teal/purple/blue at edges and shadows
- **Lighting:** Dramatic studio backlighting, internal glow through translucent surfaces, high contrast
- **Perspective:** Extreme macro, tight crop, fills frame
- **Feel:** Bold, tactile, scientific wonder — NOT tech cliché

### What It Is NOT

- ❌ CGI renders of molecules, neural networks, glowing brains
- ❌ Abstract geometric shapes without physical texture
- ❌ Stock "AI art" — circuits, data streams, grid landscapes
- ❌ Gradient blobs or motion blur abstracts without material identity
- ❌ Anything that looks illustrated or digitally painted

### AI Image Prompts (Midjourney / DALL-E / Firefly)

**For crystalline / gemstone abstract:**
```
macro photography of a faceted crystalline mineral, translucent amber and deep red internal refraction, teal and purple reflections at edges, dramatic black studio background, extreme close-up, backlit, high contrast, photorealistic, no CGI, no text
```

**For materials surface abstract:**
```
extreme macro photograph of a polished material surface with geometric facets, warm orange-gold light transmission, cool blue-purple shadows, pure black background, studio lighting, sharp focus at center, photorealistic material science photography
```

**For cover slide hero image:**
```
large translucent gemstone crystal, faceted surfaces refracting orange-amber and deep crimson light, subtle teal and violet edge reflections, floating on pure black background, dramatic backlit studio photograph, sharp edges, photorealistic, 16:9 wide composition with object on right third
```

**Colour target for abstract images:**
- Dominant warm tones: `#FF3D00` orange-red, `#F7B833` amber-gold
- Accent cool tones: `#9395DE` lavender, `#B0E1DC` teal — only at edges/shadows
- Background: `#000000` or near-black `#0A0A0A`
- Avoid: green, pink, brown, any desaturated or pastel palette

### Sourcing

- Commission from AC communications team (preferred)
- Getty/Unsplash: search "macro crystal", "gemstone macro", "mineral refraction", "material surface macro"
- Shoot your own: use macro lens, dark background, coloured gels on backlight

### When to Use

- Title/cover slide: one large hero abstract on right half of black slide
- Section divider slides: full-bleed or large partial fill on dark background
- Never on white-background content slides

## Type 2 — Photography (Science)

### What It Looks Like (from brand guide frames)

Real photos of real science. No staging that looks fake.

**Subject categories observed in AC brand guide:**
- Robotic laboratory arms / automation equipment
- Scientists with pipettes, microscopes, lab instruments
- Microscopy output images (fluorescence, brightfield) — cyan/magenta cell stains
- Close-up of materials: pills, creams, powders, surfaces
- Scientific instruments with indicator lighting (blue glow on instrument)
- Computer screens showing code or data
- Wind turbines / renewable energy infrastructure
- Clean white-on-white material macro (cream, powder, capsules)

### Visual Style

- **Lighting:** Natural or clean studio. NOT moody/dark for science shots — well-lit, professional
- **Colour:** Natural, not heavily graded. Allow some editorial warmth.
- **Composition:** Clean, uncluttered. Subject fills majority of frame.
- **Cropping:** Square crops work well for grid layouts (3×3, 3×4)
- **Background:** Lab environments, equipment, clean surfaces — not black for photography type

### AI Image Prompts

**For lab automation:**
```
robotic laboratory arm in a clean white laboratory, professional science photography, natural lighting, sharp focus, no people, real equipment, editorial style, photorealistic
```

**For scientist at work:**
```
scientist in white lab coat using a pipette at a laboratory bench, candid editorial photography, warm natural window light, shallow depth of field, professional science journal photography style
```

**For microscopy imagery:**
```
fluorescence microscopy image of cells, cyan and magenta cell staining, high contrast dark background, scientific imaging aesthetic, sharp focus, photorealistic microscopy output
```

**For materials close-up:**
```
top-down macro photograph of [material: white cream / capsule pills / powder / polymer film], clean white or grey background, sharp focus, professional product photography style, scientific
```

### Sourcing

- AC SharePoint library (preferred — use existing commissioned photos)
- Getty Images: "laboratory automation", "scientist microscope", "fluorescence microscopy", "materials science"
- Unsplash: search terms above — verify licence for commercial use
- Do NOT use: shutterstock "scientist holding molecule" style — too staged/cliché

### Grid Layout Rules

When using multiple photos together (common in AC decks):
- Use consistent square crop (1:1) for all images in a grid
- Thin black gap between images (2–4px equivalent)
- 3-column grids standard; 2-column for larger feature images
- Mix scales: 1 large image + 2–4 smaller works well (see brand guide frame 15)

## Type 3 — Diversity (People)

### What It Looks Like (from brand guide frames)

Real people, real moments. Authenticity over polish.

- Group shots at conferences, seminars, poster sessions
- Candid interaction — people talking, collaborating, presenting
- Lab environments with people working
- Mix of professional and informal settings
- Ethnically and gender diverse — reflect actual AC community

### Visual Style

- **Tone:** Warm, candid, editorial. NOT corporate headshots.
- **Lighting:** Available light or soft fill. Natural environments.
- **Moment:** Capture interaction — eye contact between people, mid-conversation, engaged
- **NOT:** Posed smiling-at-camera shots, stock photo "diversity" that looks fake

### AI Image Prompts

Note: AI-generated people carry significant risk of looking artificial. **Strongly prefer real photographs.** If AI generation is necessary:

```
candid editorial photograph of diverse group of researchers in a laboratory, natural conversation, authentic moment, soft natural lighting, photojournalistic style, no posed smiling
```

### Sourcing

- AC communications team — preferred and required for official materials
- AC SharePoint diversity folder (SDL tour, Accelerate, event photos)
- Do NOT use generic stock diversity images — they look inauthentic

## Placement Rules (Slides)

| Slide type | Recommended image use |
|---|---|
| Title / cover | Abstract (right half of black slide) |
| Section divider | Abstract full-bleed or large crop on dark BG |
| Results / data | Science photography LEFT side; chart/data RIGHT |
| Methods | Science photography (equipment/workflow) — optional |
| Team / about | Diversity grid |
| Conclusions | No image — text only on dark BG |

**Never:**
- Place image on white content slide as decoration
- Use image without direct relevance to slide content
- Use more than one "abstract" style image per section

## Composition for Slides (13.33" × 7.50")

### Cover layout
```
[LEFT HALF — black]          [RIGHT HALF — abstract image]
 Logo (top-left)              Crystal/material hero
 Eyebrow text                 Floats on black, no hard edge
 Title (orange, large)        Slight glow/vignette at boundary
 Author / date
```

### Content slide with image
```
[LEFT ~55% — image]           [RIGHT ~40% — text]
 Science photo or              Orange action title
 microscopy output             Black sub-heading
 (tight crop, fills zone)      Body bullets
                               Citation (bottom)
```

### Dark asset slide (abstract showcase)
```
[LEFT ~30% — text]            [RIGHT ~65% — abstract image grid]
 ● SECTION LABEL               2–3 abstract images tiled
 Orange title                  Dark gaps between images
 White body                    All on black background
```

## Technical Specs

| Use | Resolution | Format | Aspect |
|---|---|---|---|
| Cover hero | ≥ 2000px wide | PNG or JPG | ~1:1 or 4:3 (crops to fit) |
| Content photo | ≥ 1200px wide | PNG or JPG | 1:1 or 16:9 |
| Grid photos | ≥ 800px per cell | JPG | 1:1 square |
| Full-bleed background | ≥ 2560×1440 | JPG | 16:9 |

- Save PNG for images with transparency or sharp edges
- JPG for photographs (90% quality minimum)
- Do not use GIF or WebP in .pptx files — compatibility issues

## Quick Reference: Do / Don't

| ✅ Do | ❌ Don't |
|---|---|
| Macro crystal/gemstone with orange-gold tones | AI neural network / glowing brain clichés |
| Real lab photos — equipment, scientists at work | Illustrated / cartoon / clip art style |
| Candid authentic people shots | Posed smiling stock "diversity" |
| Dark background for abstract images | Abstract imagery on white content slides |
| 1:1 grid crops, consistent treatment | Mixed aspect ratios in same grid |
| Images that illuminate the science being discussed | Decorative images unrelated to slide content |
| Contact AC comms for commissioned assets | Altering or cropping the AC logo |

## AI Generation Quick-Reference Card

| Goal | Core prompt elements |
|---|---|
| Abstract crystal (cover) | `macro crystal, amber-gold refraction, teal edge shadows, black background, backlit, photorealistic, 16:9` |
| Abstract material (divider) | `extreme macro material surface, geometric facets, orange-red warm light, cool purple shadows, black BG` |
| Lab robot | `robotic arm, white laboratory, editorial photography, clean, professional` |
| Scientist | `scientist at bench, candid, white lab coat, natural light, photojournalistic` |
| Microscopy output | `fluorescence microscopy, cells, cyan magenta stain, dark background, scientific imaging` |
| Materials macro | `overhead macro [material], clean white background, sharp focus, product photography` |

**Always add to any prompt:**  `no text, no watermark, photorealistic, no illustration style`

**Negative prompt (if tool supports):**  `illustration, cartoon, CGI render, neural network visualization, circuit board, glowing lines, brain, molecule model, stock photo pose`
