# SDL / AC Futuristic Scientific Automation Illustration Skill

## Purpose
This skill defines the visual language for generating premium futuristic scientific automation illustrations for:
- self-driving laboratories
- robotics workflows
- organoid automation
- tissue culture systems
- laboratory infrastructure diagrams
- biotech presentation slides
- industrial scientific concept renders

The style combines:
- industrial CAD rendering
- futuristic product visualization
- technical concept art
- scientific infographic aesthetics
- high-end engineering illustration

---

# Core Style Identity

## Overall Look
- clean
- minimal
- futuristic
- highly engineered
- modular
- premium scientific aesthetic
- organized and readable
- visually balanced
- presentation-ready

The generated images should feel like:
- next-generation biotech infrastructure
- advanced robotics platform
- scientific industrial design concept
- autonomous laboratory systems

Avoid:
- cyberpunk overload
- messy industrial environments
- excessive sci-fi holograms
- fantasy machinery
- cartoon rendering
- gaming aesthetics

---

# Perspective & Composition

## Camera Angle
Preferred:
- isometric
- 3/4 top-down perspective
- orthographic-inspired composition

Avoid:
- dramatic cinematic angles
- fisheye distortion
- extreme perspective

## Composition Rules
- highly organized layout
- strong negative space
- modular structures
- clear mechanical readability
- balanced visual hierarchy
- compact but scalable system design

## Spatial Organization
Use:
- aligned modules
- interconnected workcells
- clean mechanical spacing
- structured engineering layouts

Avoid:
- clutter
- chaotic cable routing
- overlapping unreadable structures
